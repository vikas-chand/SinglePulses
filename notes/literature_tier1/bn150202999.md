# bn150202999 — GRB 150202B (literature candidates)

## Duan & Wang 2020, ApJ 890, 90 ('A Spectral Analysis of Fermi-LLE GRBs')
- id: arXiv:1912.10587
- models: Band function only (time-resolved and time-integrated); Pearson correlations among Ep, alpha, flux
- findings: Membership VERIFIED by grepping the arXiv source: 150202B is one of 36 GBM+LAT+LLE bursts, fit with n0,n1,b0 in 7 time bins. Peak-flux slice (8.06-8.79 s): Band alpha=-0.77+/-0.06, beta=-1.87+/-0.07, Ep=383+/-54 keV; integrated 0-50 s: alpha=-0.75+/-0.04, beta=-2.26+/-0.17, Ep=235+/-18 keV. Ep roughly tracks flux (Ep-F r=0.72) but alpha ANTI-tracks flux (r=-0.48) - 150202B is one of only TWO bursts in the sample (with 170115B) showing alpha anti-tracking - and Ep-alpha is strongly negative (r=-0.69); they also class it among bursts expected to show negative spectral lags.

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB Catalog, 2FLGC)
- id: arXiv:1906.11403
- models: Power law for LAT likelihood spectra (joint LAT/LLE catalog analysis)
- findings: Membership VERIFIED via HEASARC fermilgrb TAP query (entry GRB150202999, row 211): LLE detection at ~20 sigma (LLE T90~12.6 s), standard LAT likelihood TS=29 with time-integrated photon index -2.17+/-0.39, highest-energy photon ~1 GeV at ~116 s post-trigger; boresight angle 17 deg, no redshift. A weak-to-moderate GeV detection - no extended-emission BPL/SPL temporal fit values are populated for this burst.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ 913, 60 (Fermi-GBM spectral catalog, 10 yr)
- id: arXiv:2103.13528
- models: Catalog model comparison: PL / Comptonized(CPL) / Band / SBPL for fluence and peak-flux spectra
- findings: Catalog entry (verified via the HEASARC fermigbrst table, which carries these fits): fluence-spectrum best-fit model is Band with alpha=-0.83, beta=-2.15, Ep=234 keV (CPL alternative Ep=286 keV, index=-0.90). Catalog-level time-integrated fit only; no burst-specific discussion in the paper text (grep of the arXiv source found no individual mention).

## von Kienlin et al. 2020, ApJ 893, 46 (Fourth Fermi-GBM GRB Catalog)
- id: arXiv:2002.11460
- models: Catalog entry (durations/localizations; spectral fits deferred to the companion spectral catalog)
- findings: Catalog entry: T90=167.4+/-3.4 s (long, multi-episode burst), GBM fluence 3.25e-5 erg/cm2 (10-1000 keV). GCN-level context (not a paper): GBM team fit episode 1 with Band (Ep=253+/-12 keV, alpha=-0.82, beta=-2.36) and episode 2 with Comptonized (Ep=154+/-36 keV); Konus-Wind independently measured Band alpha=-0.75, beta=-2.39, Ep=234 keV for the main pulse.

## Relevance to our program
Duan & Wang's Band-only time-resolved study bears directly on our Ep/alpha evolution classification (Ep flux-tracking, r=0.72) and flags this burst as a rare alpha ANTI-tracker (one of 2/36) - directly relevant to our IT/HTS rise-phase statistics and alpha-vs-synchrotron-limit question; the 2FLGC LLE/GeV detection (PL index -2.17, ~1 GeV max photon) touches our high-energy-cutoff/second-power-law-component question but only PL fits exist. No published 2SBPL low-energy-break or blackbody test exists for this burst (verified absent from Ravasio+2019, Li+2021 multipulse, and Macera+2025 samples), so our two-break/BB time-resolved analysis would be new territory here.
