# bn150902733 — GRB 150902A (literature candidates)

## Nawaz & Sajjad 2022, MNRAS 516, 4180-4193 ('The spectral analysis of GRB 150902A and the nature of its outflow')
- id: DOI 10.1093/mnras/stac2217 (no arXiv preprint found via arXiv API; ADS bibcode would be 2022MNRAS.516.4180N — derived from confirmed volume/page, verify)
- models: Band (primary), Band+BB (sub-dominant blackbody), CPL, SBPL, additional power-law components; fit 8 keV-100 GeV (GBM+LAT)
- findings: The only dedicated spectral study of this burst: in BOTH time-integrated and time-resolved analyses (3 coarse intervals: 3.84-8.2, 8.2-10.8, 10.8-17.4 s) the fit is significantly improved by adding a sub-dominant blackbody to the Band function. Using the thermal component they constrain the outflow: strong initial magnetic content (thermal power fraction eps_Th <= 0.5); small initial radii favor internal shocks and high-efficiency magnetic reconnection is excluded; larger initial radii allow either mechanism. They liken the burst to GRB 090902B-type photospheric cases.

## He, Tam, Long, Pal, Zhang & Zhang 2022 (accepted 2021), A&A 657, A111 ('Searching for an additional high-energy component in Fermi-LAT GRB afterglows')
- id: arXiv:2109.05177 (DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202040039)
- models: Power-law vs broken power-law fits to the 0.1-200 GeV LAT afterglow spectra (not prompt GBM models)
- findings: GRB 150902A is in their Table 1 as a burst with >=10 GeV photons arriving after T90; it is one of six bursts showing tentative/marginal preference for a broken power-law over a power-law (Delta TS > 3) with a possible upturn above the break energy. The authors stress the evidence is inconclusive: no conclusive evidence that an additional higher-energy component commonly exists in LAT GRB afterglows.

- findings: bn150902733 falls squarely in the catalog's 2008-2018 coverage; the HEASARC fermigbrst row (which serves these spectral fits) gives Band as the preferred model for both fluence and peak-flux spectra (Ep ~ 346 keV, alpha ~ -0.54, beta ~ -2.25). No blackbody or multi-component models are fit in this catalog by construction.

## Relevance to our program
Nawaz & Sajjad's Band+BB detection in both time-integrated and (coarse, 3-bin) time-resolved fits directly bears on our blackbody/second-component (Ep-kT, Band+BB vs 2SBPL degeneracy) questions for bn150902733 — a top-decile-brightness burst in our sample — and gives an external benchmark our finer Bayesian-block fits should confront; their outflow-magnetization inference also connects to our curvature/photosphere interpretation, and the catalog alpha ~ -0.54 (harder than the synchrotron slow-cooling line) feeds our low-energy-index argument. Nobody has published a low-energy-break (2SBPL) or prompt high-energy-cutoff analysis of this burst, so our two-break test is novel here; He et al.'s marginal LAT spectral upturn touches the extra high-energy-component question but only in the afterglow phase.
