# bn130310840 — GRB 130310A (GBM trigger 130310840 / 384638984, 2013-03-10 20:09:41.50 UT; short/hard FRED-like burst with precursor; GBM-catalog T90 = 16.0 s in 50-300 keV, but dedicated analyses quote T90 ~ 2.4 s; LAT/LLE detection at ~79 deg off-axis triggered an ARR; Konus-Wind fluence ~3e-5 erg/cm2, emission seen to ~10 MeV) (literature candidates)

## Qin, Jiang & Wang 2021, Research in Astronomy and Astrophysics, 21, 72
- id: DOI 10.1088/1674-4527/21/3/72 (ADS bibcode candidate: 2021RAA....21...72Q; no arXiv preprint found)
- models: Band; Band+BB (blackbody); BB alone for the precursor
- findings: Dedicated time-resolved GBM+LAT spectral study: extremely high peak energy in the first time bin (Ep ~ 8.5-5.2 MeV for Band+BB, ~ 11.1-7.4 MeV for Band alone), declining to ~1 MeV; a subdominant thermal component in the early main-burst spectra with kT ~ 7-5 keV interpreted as photospheric emission, while the precursor is well fit by a BB with kT ~ 45 keV (hotter than the main burst). They interpret the burst as a transition from thermal to non-thermal radiation, i.e. a jet-composition change from fireball to Poynting-flux-dominated, with the non-thermal emission requiring an optically thin origin.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ, 913, 60 (Fermi GBM spectral catalog, 10 yr) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:2103.13528
- models: Catalog suite: PL, Comptonized (CPL), Band, SBPL fit to time-integrated (fluence) and peak-flux spectra
- findings: bn130310840 catalog entry (values confirmed in the HEASARC fermigbrst table): fluence-spectrum best-fit model is SBPL and peak-flux best-fit is Band, with the fluence Band-fit Epeak ~ 2501 keV — an extreme-Ep, very hard burst. Time-integrated fits only; no time-resolved or multi-component analysis.

## von Kienlin et al. 2020, ApJ, 893, 46 (4th Fermi GBM GRB catalog, decade of data) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:2002.11460
- models: None beyond catalog duration/localization products (spectral fits live in the companion spectral catalog)
- findings: bn130310840 catalog entry with T90 = 16.0 s (50-300 keV) — notably longer than the ~2.4 s quoted in dedicated analyses, presumably because the precursor and/or tail is included; standard trigger/duration/fluence bookkeeping only.

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ, 878, 52 (2FLGC, Second Fermi-LAT GRB catalog) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:1906.11403 (ADS: 2019ApJ...878...52A)
- models: LAT power-law likelihood analysis / LLE Bayesian-blocks detection (catalog standard)
- findings: GRB130310840 is confirmed present in the 2FLGC table (verified via HEASARC fermilgrb). Per the LAT GCN the burst was ~79 deg off-boresight and the prompt emission was detected in the non-standard LLE data at >10 sigma, prompting an autonomous repointing; i.e. an LLE-type LAT detection with high-energy (tens of MeV) prompt emission.

## Minaev & Pozanenko 2017, Astronomy Letters, 43, 1 (Precursors of short GRBs in SPI-ACS/INTEGRAL)
- id: arXiv:1612.02418 (ADS: 2017AstL...43....1M)
- models: Primarily temporal (light-curve) analysis using SPI-ACS, GBM and LAT data; no spectral-model fitting claimed in the abstract
- findings: GRB 130310 is one of their individually studied precursor candidates: light curve decomposed into three components — a precursor candidate (~0.9 s, fluence/peak flux ~30x/~25x below the main phase), the main phase (~1-2.7 s), and a weak ~500 s extended-emission tail decaying as a power law with index -1.16 +/- 0.13. Search snippets also attribute to this analysis the finding of no spectral lag between the 8-200 and 200-900 keV bands, ascribed to strongly overlapping pulses (candidate claim - verify in the paper). Population-level conclusion of the paper: no convincing evidence for sGRB precursors as a class.

## Li, Shen & Zhang 2023, ApJ, 955, 98
- id: arXiv:2308.06151
- models: None (theoretical timing paper: Lense-Thirring jet precession in BH-NS mergers; no spectral fitting)
- findings: Proposes that BH-NS mergers imprint a delayed quasi-periodic modulation (quasi-period ~0.01-0.1 s) on sGRB light curves via Lense-Thirring precession, and states in the abstract that this modulation 'may have already revealed itself in a few observed sGRBs (such as GRB 130310A)' — i.e. GRB 130310A is flagged as a candidate precessing BH-NS jet. Timing/temporal claim only, no spectroscopy.

## Relevance to our program
Qin et al. 2021 directly touches our blackbody/second-component question: they claim a subdominant BB (kT ~ 5-7 keV) under a Band component in time-resolved fits plus a purely thermal (kT ~ 45 keV) precursor — a direct comparison point for our Band+BB fits, Ep-kT track, and thermal-to-nonthermal jet-composition framing; the extreme Ep (~2.5-11 MeV) also stresses our fit bounds. Crucially, NO published study has tested a low-energy break (2SBPL/DSBPL) on this burst — the GBM catalog's SBPL fluence preference and the FRED single-pulse-with-precursor morphology (plus the QPO/precession and 500-s extended-emission claims for peak-width/variability context) make it a fresh target for our two-break and curvature analysis.
