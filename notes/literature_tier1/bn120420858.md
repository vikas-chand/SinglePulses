## Relevance to our program
GRB 120420B has NO dedicated published spectral study, no GCN circulars found, and is row-verified ABSENT from the Yu+2016 GBM time-resolved pulse-spectroscopy catalog and the Li 2021 multi-pulse catalog — it exists only as GBM catalog entries, so our time-resolved analysis would be the first for this burst and there are no published claims about low-energy breaks, blackbody components, or a second component to compare against. The one hook to our questions: the catalog time-integrated Band fit has an anomalously shallow beta (~ -1.64) with Band-vs-Comptonized best-model ambiguity (fluence vs peak-flux spectrum), which touches our curvature/high-energy-cutoff and model-selection (Band vs CPL vs 2SBPL) questions directly.
