# bn171210493 — GRB 171210A (literature candidates)

## von Kienlin et al. 2020, ApJ 893, 46 (Fourth Fermi-GBM GRB Catalog: A Decade of Data)
- id: arXiv:2002.11460
- models: Catalog entry (durations/localizations/fluences; detailed spectral fits in companion spectral catalog)
- findings: GRB 171210A = bn171210493 catalog entry: single bright peak, T90 ~143 s (50-300 keV), high peak flux triggering an Autonomous Repoint Request (per GBM team GCN 22233; catalog covers all GBM triggers 2008-2018 so inclusion is by construction). No burst-specific discussion.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ 913, 60 (Fermi GBM Gamma-Ray Burst Spectral Catalog: 10 Years of Data)
- id: arXiv:2103.13528
- models: Band, Comptonized (CPL), power law, SBPL fitted to time-integrated fluence and 1-s peak-flux spectra
- findings: Catalog-level time-integrated fits only (no time-resolved analysis). Verified via the HEASARC fermigbrst table that underlies this catalog: fluence-spectrum best fit = Band with Ep ~139 keV, alpha ~ -0.76, beta ~ -2.39; peak-flux-spectrum best fit = Comptonized with Ep ~163 keV.

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (A Decade of Gamma-Ray Bursts Observed by Fermi-LAT: The 2nd GRB Catalog, 2FLGC)
- id: arXiv:1906.11403
- models: Power law (with PL+cutoff/comparison fits per catalog methodology) for 100 MeV-100 GeV; LLE (30-100 MeV) analysis
- findings: GRB 171210A is included in the 2FLGC as a LAT/LLE-detected burst (search-confirmed membership). HEASARC fermilgrb catalog row: LLE detection significance ~14 sigma with LLE T90 ~8.2 s, >100 MeV emission lasting ~1.5 ks (TL100 ~1512 s), likelihood TS ~65 — i.e., delayed/extended high-energy emission typical of the catalog sample.

## He, Tam, Long, Pal, Zhang & Zhang 2022, A&A 657, A111 (Searching for an additional high-energy component in Fermi-LAT GRB afterglows)
- id: arXiv:2109.05177
- models: Power law vs broken power law (upturn above a break energy) fitted to Fermi-LAT afterglow-phase spectra of 199 LAT GRBs
- findings: GRB 171210A is listed among six bursts (with 190114C, 150902A, 130907A, 130427A, 090902B) where the BPL improvement over PL is only 'tentative or marginal' (Delta TS > 3), i.e., a possible upturn above the break energy in its LAT afterglow spectrum; overall the paper finds no conclusive evidence that an extra high-energy component is common.

## Relevance to our program
GRB 171210A appears only in catalogs and LAT sample studies — no dedicated paper and no published time-resolved prompt-emission spectroscopy found, so our single-pulse time-resolved analysis (low-energy break / 2SBPL vs Band, blackbody search, peak-width evolution) would be untested territory for this burst. The existing literature touches our questions only obliquely: the GBM catalog's time-integrated Band fit (alpha ~ -0.76, Ep ~139 keV) sits between the synchrotron limits with no BB or low-energy-break model tried, while the 2FLGC and He et al. 2022 address the high-energy/second-component question (14-sigma LLE detection, ~1.5 ks GeV emission, tentative spectral upturn) but in the afterglow phase, not the prompt spectrum.
