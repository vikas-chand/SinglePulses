# bn151006413 — GRB 151006A (literature candidates)

## Basak, Iyyani, Chand, Chattopadhyay, Bhattacharya, Rao & Vadawale 2017, MNRAS 472, 891 ('Surprise in simplicity: an unusual spectral evolution of a single pulse GRB 151006A')
- id: arXiv:1707.09924 / 2017MNRAS.472..891B
- models: Band; Band+blackbody; two blackbodies + power law (2BBPL); joint Fermi GBM + Swift fits, complemented by CZTI polarization
- findings: Despite a simple single FRED pulse, the spectral evolution is unusual and two-phased: hard-to-soft evolution until ~16-20 s, then a sudden increase of the spectral peak to a few MeV, coincident with the onset of Fermi-LAT high-energy photons (first LAT photon at t~17.5 s). They interpret the late hardening as either nascent afterglow emission or a hidden second hard pulse within the prompt phase — reported for the first time in a single-pulse burst.

## Rao et al. 2016, ApJ 833, 86 ('AstroSat CZT Imager Observations of GRB 151006A: Timing, Spectroscopy, and Polarization Study')
- id: arXiv:1608.07388 / 2016ApJ...833...86R
- models: Band; cutoff power law; blackbody + power law; two blackbodies + power law (2BBPL) — joint CZTI/GBM/BAT fits
- findings: First-light AstroSat CZTI GRB; CZTI in conjunction with Swift yields spectral parameters consistent with Fermi/GBM, with the Band model the preferred fit in most intervals (BB+PL and 2BBPL also tested). Despite being relatively faint, the burst shows hints of a polarization signal at 100-300 keV, though at low significance.

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB Catalog, 2FLGC) [catalog entry]
- id: arXiv:1906.11403
- models: Standard 2FLGC likelihood analysis (power-law LAT spectral fits; LLE detection machinery)
- findings: GRB 151006A is included in the 2FLGC (verified by grepping the catalog PDF: entry '151006A (L)'), flagged as an LLE-band detection with tabulated LAT-era durations; i.e., it has cataloged >30 MeV emission consistent with the LAT photons discussed by Basak et al. 2017.

## Relevance to our program
Directly in our regime: a single-pulse FRED long GRB (likely in our 106-burst sample) with dedicated time-resolved spectroscopy. Basak et al. 2017's anomalous late-time Ep jump to MeV energies coincident with LAT/LLE onset bears on our second-component/saddle and Ep-tracking questions; Rao et al. 2016's BB+PL and 2BBPL fits bear on our blackbody (Ep-kT) question. Notably, NO published low-energy-break (2SBPL/DSBPL-style) analysis of this burst was found, so our two-break fits and any curvature classification would be new; no high-energy-cutoff claim appears either.
