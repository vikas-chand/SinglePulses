# bn130518580 — GRB 130518A (literature candidates)

## Siddique, Sajjad & Motiwala 2022, ApJ, 938, 159
- id: 2022ApJ...938..159S (DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ac8d05; no arXiv preprint found)
- models: Band, CPL, SBPL, each also tried + power-law and + blackbody components; joint Fermi GBM+LAT; time-integrated best fit = SBPL+BB; time-resolved analysis in 5 bins
- findings: The time-integrated GBM+LAT spectrum is nonthermal with a subdominant blackbody component (SBPL+BB preferred); in the 5-bin time-resolved analysis the BB is significant during the peak emission (19.39-35.52 s). Applying the Hascoet et al. and Gao & Zhang hybrid-jet frameworks, the pure fireball model is excluded, the initial magnetic fraction exceeds the thermal fraction in all viable cases, and internal shocks are favored for small jet launching radii.

## Dirirsa et al. 2019, ApJ, 887, 13
- id: arXiv:1910.07009 (2019ApJ...887...13D)
- models: Band, SBPL, CPL, with additional power-law components where required; joint GBM (n3+n6+n7+b0+b1) + LAT fits over the T90 window
- findings: For GRB 130518A their joint GBM+LAT time-integrated fit over 9.9-57.9 s prefers a plain Band function with alpha=-0.89+/-0.01, beta=-2.71+/-0.03, Ep=458.85+/-9.22 keV (no extra PL component, unlike 130427A); with z=2.49 they derive Ep,i=1601.4+/-32.2 keV and Eiso=1.67e54 erg and place the burst on the Amati relation. It is one of six Fermi GRBs common with the earlier Amati samples in their cosmology analysis.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ, 913, 60 (Fermi-GBM Spectral Catalog: 10 yr of Data) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:2103.13528 (2021ApJ...913...60P)
- models: PL, CPL (Comptonized), Band, SBPL; time-integrated (fluence) and peak-flux fits
- findings: Catalog entry for bn130518580 (verified in HEASARC fermigbrst): the time-integrated (fluence) spectrum is best fit by SBPL while the peak-flux spectrum prefers Band; the fluence Band fit gives alpha=-0.86, beta=-2.18, Ep=381 keV, with T90=48.6 s. Standard catalog fits only — no burst-specific discussion.

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ, 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB Catalog, 2FLGC) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:1906.11403 (2019ApJ...878...52A)
- models: Power-law (and PL+cutoff where warranted) fits to the LAT >100 MeV emission; LLE detection analysis; joint GBM-LAT temporal comparison
- findings: GRB130518580 is confirmed present in the 2FLGC (verified in the HEASARC fermilgrb table), i.e. it is a LAT-detected burst with high-energy (>100 MeV) emission characterized by the catalog's standard likelihood analysis (durations, fluence, PL spectral index of the high-energy component). Per-burst values were not extracted in this sweep.

## Tsvetkova et al. 2017, ApJ, 850, 161 (Konus-Wind catalog of GRBs with known redshifts I, triggered mode) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:1710.08746 (2017ApJ...850..161T)
- models: Band and exponential-cutoff power-law fits over ~20 keV-15 MeV (time-integrated and peak spectra)
- findings: GRB 130518A (z=2.488) is in the 150-burst triggered-mode Konus-Wind sample (row verified in Vizier table J/ApJ/850/161), with broad-band spectral fits and rest-frame energetics (Eiso ~2e54 erg class) used in their Amati/Yonetoku correlation analysis. Catalog-level analysis only.

## Relevance to our program
Siddique+2022 directly touches our blackbody/second-component question: a subdominant BB on top of a nonthermal (SBPL) continuum, significant only around the pulse peak — exactly the Band+BB / CPL+BB vs curvature-model degeneracy our ΔAIC framework probes; note they tested SBPL+BB but NOT 2SBPL, so a low-energy-break (two-break) interpretation of this burst is untested in the literature. The catalog-level SBPL-vs-Band tension (Poolakkil: SBPL wins time-integrated, Band at peak; Dirirsa: Band with steep beta=-2.71) bears on our low-energy-break and peak-sharpness questions, and the LAT/2FLGC membership plus Dirirsa's no-extra-PL result addresses the high-energy cutoff/second-component side. No published time-resolved Ep-kT track or 2SBPL fit exists, leaving room for our pipeline.
