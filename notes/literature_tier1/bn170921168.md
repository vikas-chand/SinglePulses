# bn170921168 — GRB 170921B (literature candidates)

## Li & Zhang 2021, ApJS 253, 43
- id: arXiv:2101.04325 (doi 10.3847/1538-4365/abded1)
- models: Time-resolved decay-phase fits with simple power law (PL) and cutoff power law (CPL); CPL preferred over PL for the cases shown
- findings: Systematic temporal+spectral search for the high-latitude curvature effect in single-pulse Fermi/GBM bursts. GRB 170921168 is in their Table 1 (long burst, duration ~39.4 s); per the search snippets it is NOT in the Phase I sample but IS in the Phase II sample, i.e., the curvature effect may only dominate the later part of its decay (snippets also flag it among bursts deviating from simple HLE predictions — verify against the paper). Population conclusion: HLE identified in a subsample, emission radius ~1e15-1e16 cm, evidence of bulk acceleration favoring Poynting-flux-dominated jets.

## von Kienlin et al. 2020, ApJ 893, 46 (4th Fermi-GBM GRB catalog)
- id: arXiv:2002.11460
- models: Catalog entry (durations/fluence/peak flux); detailed spectral fits are in the companion spectral catalog
- findings: bn170921168 explicitly appears in this decade catalog (confirmed by search hit on the arXiv PDF). Standard catalog-level characterization only — no burst-specific discussion.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ 913, 60 (Fermi-GBM 10-yr spectral catalog)
- id: arXiv:2103.13528
- models: PL, Comptonized (CPL), Band, and SBPL fit to time-integrated (fluence) and peak-flux spectra
- findings: CANDIDATE catalog entry: inclusion inferred from coverage (same 2008-2018 trigger window as the 4th catalog, which explicitly lists bn170921168); the specific best-fit row for this burst was not retrieved in this sweep and should be verified against the machine-readable table. GCN-level quicklook (Veres, GCN 21902) had Band with Ep=100+/-10 keV, alpha=-1.2, beta=-2.3, fluence 5.4e-5 erg/cm2.

## Relevance to our program
No dedicated spectral paper exists for this burst, so our time-resolved analysis would be new. The one real study (Li & Zhang 2021) treats it as a clean bright single pulse and touches our curvature/HLE question directly — they place its DECAY in the curvature-effect (Phase II) class, which bears on our two-break/curvature classification and peak-width work. Nothing published addresses a low-energy break, blackbody component, or high-energy cutoff for this burst beyond catalog Band/CPL fits; GCN-level Konus-Wind fits (CPL/Band, Ep ~318 keV near peak vs ~138 keV time-averaged, plus a ~164 s decay tail) indicate strong hard-to-soft Ep evolution relevant to our Ep-tracking and saddle/second-component questions.
