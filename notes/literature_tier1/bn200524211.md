# bn200524211 — GRB 200524A (literature candidates)

## Poolakkil, Mailyan & Hamburg 2020, GCN Circular 27809 (Fermi GBM team) — catalog-entry level, not refereed
- id: 2020GCN.27809....1P
- models: Band (time-averaged, T0-1.5 to T0+25.1 s)
- findings: Time-averaged GBM spectrum best fit by Band with Epeak = 192 +/- 16 keV, alpha = -0.66 +/- 0.06, beta = -1.77 +/- 0.03; fluence (10-1000 keV) = (2.08 +/- 0.03)e-5 erg/cm2; T90 ~ 38 s (50-300 keV), multi-peaked light curve. Same fit propagates to the online HEASARC GBM burst catalog entry (the burst post-dates the published 10-yr GBM spectral catalog, which ends July 2018).

## Svinkin et al. 2020, GCN Circular 27867 (Konus-Wind team) — catalog-entry level, not refereed
- id: 2020GCN.27867....1S
- models: Band (GRB function), time-averaged + near-peak intervals
- findings: Konus-Wind time-averaged (T0 to T0+40.7 s) Band fit: alpha = -0.75(-0.18,+0.25), beta = -2.13(-0.31,+0.20), Ep = 215(-46,+48) keV; near-peak (T0+0.256 to T0+7.936 s): alpha = -0.55, beta = -2.14, Ep = 203 +/- 36 keV; fluence 3.48e-5 erg/cm2, duration ~89 s multipeaked.

## Fermi-LAT team 2020, GCN Circular 27797 — detection circular, not refereed
- id: 2020GCN.27797....1F
- models: None (LAT >100 MeV detection report; no spectral fit quoted in snippets)
- findings: LAT detection at >100 MeV coincident with the GBM burst; highest-energy photon 9.2 GeV observed 748 s after trigger — establishes a GeV component extending well past the prompt phase. Burst post-dates the published 2FLGC catalog (ends 2018).

## Ho et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 85 (Cosmological Fast Optical Transients with ZTF: A Search for Dirty Fireballs)
- id: arXiv:2201.12366
- models: None for the prompt emission (afterglow photometry/spectroscopy only; power-law afterglow analysis)
- findings: Refereed paper covering the ZTF afterglow AT 2020kym of GRB 200524A: 18 mag/day intranight fading, z = 1.256 from Gemini-GMOS Mg II absorption, X-ray/radio follow-up, comparison to LGRB afterglows. Explicitly does NO prompt-emission spectral fitting and defers the multiwavelength analysis to 'A. Ghosh et al. (in preparation)' — a paper that, per arXiv metadata search through July 2026, was never published (it exists only as an Oct 2021 ARIES seminar reporting reverse-forward-shock afterglow modeling with R_B ~ 1.38, partially magnetized jet).

## Relevance to our program
GRB 200524A is effectively unstudied at the level of published prompt-emission spectroscopy: no refereed time-resolved or time-integrated spectral paper exists (arXiv metadata search for "200524A" returns zero papers; it is absent from the published GBM 10-yr spectral catalog, 2FLGC, and the CZTI polarization catalog — only GCN-level Band fits exist). That means our questions — low-energy break (2SBPL) vs the GCN Band alpha = -0.66 sitting right at the -2/3 synchrotron line of death, any subdominant blackbody, and the origin/cutoff of the LAT GeV component (9.2 GeV at 748 s) — are all open, and any time-resolved analysis from our pipeline would be the first published one; note the tension between GBM's "multi-peaked" description and the ARIES claim of a single-pulse high-energy light curve, which matters for our single-pulse sample membership.
