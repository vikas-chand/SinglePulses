# bn201104001 — GRB 201104A (literature candidates)

## Wang, Jiang & Ren 2022, ApJ 935, 179
- id: arXiv:2205.02982 (DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ac82ec)
- models: Band and cutoff power law (CPL); joint Fermi-GBM (NaI n8 + BGO b1, GBM Data Tools) + Fermi-LAT spectral fits via Bayesian inference; time-integrated AND time-resolved (Bayesian-block-defined episodes a [T0-0.1,T0+2.7 s], b [T0+2.7,T0+5.5 s], c [T0+5.5,T0+8.3 s]). No blackbody or multi-component models tested.
- findings: GRB 201104A is a long-duration burst with SGRB characteristics (Amati diagram places each episode near the short-burst group) that shows 'repetitive' behavior: Episodes a and b have nearly identical posterior spectral parameters. Bayesian inference on the light curve does not fully support a gravitationally lensed SGRB, while the spectral inference is consistent with lensing; the authors conclude there is no strong evidence for lensing, leaving an intrinsic repetition mechanism (a central engine 'with memory') as the alternative. Their QPO search over 248 short + 920 long Fermi bursts found no >3-sigma QPO signal.

## Relevance to our program
The only published study fits just Band/CPL — no one has searched this burst for a low-energy break (2SBPL), a thermal/blackbody component, or a high-energy cutoff, so those questions are open territory for our pipeline; the paper does supply time-resolved Band/CPL parameters, Bayesian-block episode edges, and joint GBM+LAT fits (LAT detected it up to 3.7 GeV, GCN 28828) useful as a cross-check for our binning and any high-E cutoff claim. Caution for our single-pulse framing: the burst is multi-episode with two spectrally near-identical pulses (a possible lensed/repeated pair), i.e., not a clean single pulse, which bears directly on our peak-width and pulse-shape questions.
