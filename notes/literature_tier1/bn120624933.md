# bn120624933 — GRB 120624B (literature candidates)

## de Ugarte Postigo et al. 2013, A&A 557, A18 ("The obscured hyper-energetic GRB 120624B hosted by a luminous compact galaxy at z = 2.20")
- id: arXiv:1309.1167
- models: Band function only (time-integrated and time-resolved/per-pulse GBM spectra; LAT-range spectrum also described by the same Band function)
- findings: The dedicated multiwavelength study: GBM shows three bright pulses over T90~271 s (first starting 270 s before trigger; only the third triggered GBM), fluence (1.916+/-0.002)e-4 erg/cm2 (top 0.6% of GBM bursts), and both time-integrated and per-pulse spectra are fit with the typical Band function. Significant LAT detection >100 MeV, but the LAT-range spectrum is still well described by a Band function with 'no emerging high-energy extra component'; host spectroscopy gives z=2.1974, Eiso=(3.0+/-0.2)e54 erg.

## Yu et al. 2016, A&A 588, A135 (Fermi GBM time-resolved spectral catalog: brightest bursts in the first four years)
- id: arXiv:1601.05206
- models: Per-bin fits with the standard catalog set: power law, Comptonized (CPL), Band, SBPL (plus a catalog-wide pure-Planck blackbody search)
- findings: bn120624933 is in the catalog with 20 time-resolved bins (verified in the paper's tables, bins spanning -270 s to +48 s): best-fit models are predominantly Comptonized (CPL), with one Band bin near trigger and power-law fits in the faint inter-pulse bins; its Ep evolution is classified as intensity-tracking. Catalog-wide only 3 of 81 bursts show pure-Planck evidence, and 120624B is not among them.

## Tsvetkova et al. 2017, ApJ 850, 161 (Konus-Wind catalog of GRBs with known redshifts, Part I: triggered-mode bursts)
- id: arXiv:1710.08746
- models: Konus-Wind CPL and Band fits (catalog standard) to time-integrated and peak spectra, yielding Ep, Eiso, Liso
- findings: GRB 120624B is a confirmed sample entry (verified in Table 1: KW trigger 22:20:06.9 UT — i.e., KW triggered on the early bright pulse ~229 s before the GBM trigger — Type II, Swift/BAT localization, z=2.1974 spectroscopic, incidence angle 85.4 deg). The catalog tabulates KW spectral fits and rest-frame energetics for its sample; per-burst numbers for 120624B are in the machine-readable tables (candidate for verification against the full table).

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB Catalog, 2FLGC)
- id: arXiv:1906.11403
- models: LAT likelihood power-law spectral analysis (>100 MeV) plus LLE (30 MeV-1 GeV) detection/duration analysis (catalog standard)
- findings: GRB 120624B is a 2FLGC entry (verified in the catalog tables, T90,GBM=271.36 s) detected both >100 MeV and in LLE; the text singles it out (with GRB 150513A) because the burst triggered Swift 257 s before GBM, so its LLE onset TLLE,05 is negative (LLE emission begins ~260 s before the GBM trigger) and it is omitted from the onset-comparison figure. LAT fluxes/durations are tabulated; catalog entry, not a dedicated study.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJS 254, 17 (Fermi GBM Gamma-Ray Burst Spectral Catalog: 10 years of data)
- id: arXiv:2103.13528
- models: Catalog-standard four-model fits: power law, Comptonized (CPL), Band, SBPL, to both time-integrated (fluence) and peak-flux spectra
- findings: GRB 120624933 appears in the 10-year GBM spectral catalog (verified in the PDF's rest-frame properties table, which tabulates its Eiso/Liso/rest-frame Ep given its redshift); standard best-fit model selection between PL/COMP/Band/SBPL for fluence and peak spectra. Catalog entry only; exact per-column values should be taken from the machine-readable table (PDF column extraction was scrambled).

## Relevance to our program
Directly relevant as a bright, hyper-energetic (Eiso~3e54, z=2.20) multi-pulse test case for our low-energy-break and second-component questions, but with essentially untapped territory: no published 2SBPL/low-energy-break or blackbody analysis exists for this burst (Yu+16 finds COMP/Band-dominated bins with intensity-tracking Ep and no pure-Planck evidence; de Ugarte Postigo+13 used Band-only fits and explicitly report NO extra high-energy component, with the LAT-range spectrum Band-consistent — touching our high-E cutoff/saddle question). Two caveats for our pipeline: (1) most emission (2 of 3 pulses) occurred ~270-90 s BEFORE the GBM trigger (KW and Swift triggered first), so trigger-mode TTE covers only the final pulse; (2) beware sibling short burst GRB 120624A = bn120624309, which IS in Ravasio et al. 2019's spectral-break sample — the '120624' hit there is NOT our burst.
