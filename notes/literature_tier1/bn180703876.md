# bn180703876 — GRB 180703A (literature candidates)

## Ajello et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB Catalog, 2FLGC)
- id: arXiv:1906.11403
- models: Power law (LAT-only fits in GBM window and LAT-transient window); joint GBM+LAT spectral fits (Band/Comptonized-family, per catalog methodology)
- findings: GRB 180703A verified present in 2FLGC Tables 2-6 (PDF grep): an LLE-class detection with GBM T90 = 20.7 s, LLE T90 = 7.1 s, and LAT temporally extended emission out to ~1600 s post-trigger; the LAT power-law fit in the prompt (GBM) window is very soft (photon index ~ -3 +/- 1, few photons). Catalog entry only — no burst-specific discussion.

## Poolakkil et al. 2021, ApJ 913, 60 (Fermi GBM Spectral Catalog: 10 yr of Data)
- id: arXiv:2103.13528 / ADS 2021ApJ...913...60P
- models: Power law, Comptonized (CPL), Band, SBPL (standard four-model catalog fits, time-integrated fluence + peak-flux spectra)
- findings: Catalog entry verified via live HEASARC fermigbrst query: the time-integrated (fluence) spectrum is best fit by a Band function with Ep = 350.8 keV, alpha = -0.78, beta = -1.97, while the 1-s peak-flux spectrum is best fit by SBPL. Consistent with the GBM team GCN Band fit (Ep = 360 +/- 33 keV, alpha = -0.80, beta = -1.95, fluence ~2.0e-5 erg/cm2). Catalog entry only.

## von Kienlin et al. 2020, ApJ 893, 46 (Fourth Fermi-GBM GRB Catalog: A Decade of Data)
- id: arXiv:2002.11460 / ADS 2020ApJ...893...46V
- models: None (duration/localization/fluence catalog; spectral fits deferred to the companion spectral catalog)
- findings: Catalog entry (trigger bn180703876): long single-peak burst with T90 = 20.74 s (50-300 keV). No burst-specific analysis.

## Relevance to our program
GRB 180703A is effectively unstudied beyond catalogs: no dedicated prompt-spectroscopy paper and no time-resolved analysis exists (checked and excluded: Wang et al. 2024 RAA bright-GRB time-resolved sample, Dirirsa et al. 2019 LAT-redshift spectral sample, CZTI 5-yr polarization catalog, Konus-Wind redshift catalog I, Fraija et al. 2023 2FLGC closure-relation sample; remaining literature mentions are spectral-lag/LIV timing papers, e.g. arXiv:2210.03897 and arXiv:2307.02296, not spectroscopy). Published constraints touching our questions are catalog-level only: the Band-vs-SBPL time-integrated preference (alpha ~ -0.78, Ep ~ 351 keV) leaves our low-energy-break (2SBPL), blackbody, and high-energy-cutoff questions completely open; the 2FLGC LLE/GeV detection (max photon ~0.9 GeV, soft LAT index in the prompt window) is relevant context for any high-energy cutoff or second component; z = 0.6678 (VLT/MUSE host, GCN 23889 — circular, not refereed) enables rest-frame Ep/peak-width numbers. Our time-resolved two-break analysis of this burst would be new.
