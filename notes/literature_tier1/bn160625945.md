# bn160625945 — GRB 160625B (literature candidates)

## Ravasio et al. 2018, A&A 613, A16
- id: arXiv:1711.03106
- models: Band/CPL standard empirical functions; new 2SBPL (three smoothly connected power laws, i.e. peak + low-energy break); non-thermal+blackbody composite tested and systematically disfavored vs 2SBPL
- findings: Time-resolved GBM spectra of the main episode cannot be fit by standard functions and require a low-energy break at E_break ~ 100 keV with Epeak evolving over 0.5-6 MeV; slopes below/above the break are consistent with -0.67 and -1.5, the synchrotron values for a marginally fast-cooling electron population with a low-energy cutoff. Both Epeak and Ebreak decrease with time (hard-to-soft evolution).
- **VERIFIED (PDF pp.3-5, 2026-07-24):** main-episode interval **186.40-207.91 s** (S/N>20 in BGO1), 21x1.024s bins. 2SBPL smoothness **n2=2.69 fixed (Λ=0.3), n1=5.38 fixed**. Time-integrated: **E_break=107.8±1.9 keV, E_peak=673.5±10.8 keV, α1=-0.62, α2=-1.50**, >8σ over SBPL, 2SBPL favored over SBPL+BB. Time-resolved: 2SBPL beats SBPL at >4.8σ in 19/21 bins. With LLE: exp. cutoff **E_cut=50.3⁺⁷·⁴₋₁³·² MeV** (or 4th PL segment breaking at 27.3 MeV), γγ → Γ~200-250. Reconciliation: our blk11 (in-window) two-break recovers to xp≈534 keV once seed-railing fixed (see notes/reconciliation/bn160625945.md, L7/L8).

## Zhang, B.-B. et al. 2018, Nature Astronomy 2, 69
- id: arXiv:1612.03089
- models: Blackbody (photospheric thermal) for the precursor; Band function (non-thermal) for the main episode; time-resolved fits across all three sub-bursts
- findings: The three-episode burst shows distinctly different spectra between sub-bursts: a purely thermal component in the precursor (the bn160625945-triggered episode) and a non-thermal Band spectrum in the main episode, interpreted as the first observed transition from a fireball (photosphere) to a Poynting-flux-dominated jet within a single GRB.

## Lu (Lü), H.-J. et al. 2017, ApJ 849, 71
- id: 2017ApJ...849...71L (arXiv:1702.01382)
- models: Multicolor blackbody (mBB); mBB + cutoff power law (CPL); CPL + single power law — fitted per episode
- findings: Episode I fits a multicolor blackbody (photospheric/cocoon emission); Episode II (main, GBM+LAT) needs mBB + CPL (photosphere plus internal-shock non-thermal); Episode III fits CPL + power law with no thermal component — interpreted as long-term evolution of the ejecta from fireball-like to shock-dominated.

## Wang, Y.-Z. et al. 2017, ApJ 836, 81
- id: arXiv:1611.04879
- models: Thermal-radiation (photospheric) model and high-energy spectral-cutoff models applied to time-resolved spectra to derive bulk Lorentz factors
- findings: Lorentz factors derived from the thermal component are consistent with the luminosity-Lorentz factor correlation, whereas the spectral-cutoff method gives much lower values in tension with pair-opacity constraints; they conclude observed cutoffs at tens of MeV likely reflect particle-acceleration physics rather than gamma-gamma opacity, cautioning against using them for Lorentz-factor estimates.

## Troja, E. et al. 2017, Nature 547, 425
- id: 2017Natur.547..425T
- models: Prompt optical polarimetry combined with broadband prompt-emission modeling; fast-cooling synchrotron interpretation (not a GBM model-comparison paper)
- findings: Detected 8.3 +/- 0.8 per cent variable linear polarization of the prompt optical flash accompanying the main gamma-ray episode, arguing the prompt emission is fast-cooling synchrotron radiation in a large-scale ordered magnetic field advected from the central engine. Included as borderline-spectral: it constrains the emission mechanism rather than fitting GBM spectral models.

## Li, L., Ruffini, R., Rueda, J. A., Moradi, R., Wang, Y., Xue, S.-S. 2023, ApJ 945, 10
- id: arXiv:1910.12615 (2023ApJ...945...10L)
- models: Time-resolved GBM spectral analysis on ever-decreasing time bins within the BdHN framework (abstract does not name the fitting functions)
- findings: For four BdHN I candidates including GRB 160625B, they report self-similar spectral structures and associated power laws in the ultra-relativistic prompt emission (UPE) phase, validated by time-resolved analysis on progressively finer time steps; used to support the three-episode BdHN interpretation.

## Poolakkil, S. et al. 2021, ApJ 913, 60 (Fermi GBM spectral catalog, 10 yr) — catalog entry
- id: arXiv:2103.13528 (2021ApJ...913...60P)
- models: Standard GBM catalog functions (power law, Comptonized/CPL, Band, SBPL); time-integrated and peak-flux spectral fits
- findings: GRB 160625B appears as the third-largest-fluence burst in the catalog (5.7e-4 erg cm^-2, 10-1000 keV); the catalog provides its standard time-integrated and brightest-bin spectral fits as part of the 2297-GRB compendium.

## Ajello, M. et al. 2019, ApJ 878, 52 (Second Fermi-LAT GRB catalog) — catalog entry
- id: 2019ApJ...878...52A
- models: LAT power-law spectral fits in the 100 MeV-100 GeV band; onset/duration/temporal characterization
- findings: GRB 160625B is included in the 10-yr LAT GRB catalog with high-energy spectral and temporal properties; a GBM prompt duration of 453.38 s and Eiso ~ 1.5e53 erg (per the catalog, as quoted in later literature) are reported for the burst.

## Relevance to our program
bn160625945 is the PRECURSOR trigger of GRB 160625B (the extremely bright main episode re-triggered as bn160625952, T90 ~ 460-770 s across three episodes, z=1.406) — this is arguably the archetype burst for OUR core questions: Ravasio+18 invented the 2SBPL (two-break) function on this burst's main episode, finding E_break ~ 100 keV with synchrotron slopes -0.67/-1.5 (our low-energy-break question directly); Zhang+18 and Lü+17 establish a genuine blackbody/photospheric component in the precursor episode and its transition to non-thermal (our blackbody/second-component question); Wang+17 tests high-energy spectral cutoffs and their (non-)opacity origin (our high-E cutoff question); note Ravasio+18 explicitly found 2SBPL beats non-thermal+BB composites, the exact model competition our pipeline runs. Caveat for our single-pulse framing: the burst is multi-episode, so this trigger's episode (the ~1 s thermal precursor) and the 2SBPL-prototype main episode belong to different triggers/windows.
