## BAND

1. **Fit verdict: good.** Residuals flat and structureless in all three detectors;
   no run exceeds ±2σ; largest excursions are isolated single points (−1.8σ at 17 keV,
   −2.0σ near 140 keV).
2. **Parameters.** α = −0.123 ± 0.085 (MINOS −0.092/+0.097) — sane, hard but not railed.
   Ep = 243.3 ± 13.4 keV (−13.5/+16.3) — 5.5% error, consistent with a 46σ block.
   **β = −4.99996, railed at the −5 bound** (MINOS −3.5e−5/+1.43): the 68% interval
   reaches only up to ≈ −3.57, i.e. β is unconstrained from below. K = 0.204 ± 0.019.
   Status OK, MINOS OK, but VALID = False (the β rail). −2lnL = 1365.02, AIC 1377.02.
3. **Systematic structure:** none. Low (<30 keV): one isolated −1.8σ dip at 17 keV,
   neighbours positive — not a run. Mid (30–300 keV): short alternating-sign wobbles,
   amplitude ≤2σ, mixed NA/NB signs within the same energy ranges. High: two B1 points
   on the model within ±0.6σ; upper limits only beyond.
4. **Which range better modeled:** all three ranges equally well; no detector shows a
   preferred failure band. The high band is simply data-starved (2 B1 detections).
5. **What it suggests:** β running to the rail with only two BGO detections and upper
   limits above means the high-energy side demands a drop at least as steep as any
   power law the data can measure — the Band β is doing no work. Next hypothesis:
   **CPL family (exponential cutoff)**; test = LRT/AIC vs CPL, which the engine indeed
   resolves in CPL's favour (Δ(−2lnL) = 0.02 for one extra Band parameter).

## Comparative paragraph

All four simple continua produce **the same residuals to within a pixel** — there is no
structure for the extra parameters to absorb, and none of the model-to-model contrasts
the review grammar looks for (a weave that SBPL flattens, a shoulder that only Band's β
tolerates) exists in this bin. The statistics agree with the images: −2lnL spans just
1365.30 → 1364.49 across models with 4 → 8 free parameters, so every added parameter is
dead weight; and all three β-bearing models (Band, SBPL, DSBPL) drove β to the −5 rail,
which is the three-fold signature of a spectrum that ends in a cutoff-like drop with no
measured high-energy power law — only two B1 detections (~350, ~450 keV, both within
±0.6σ) and upper limits above. The data are featureless everywhere the models differ:
the low band shows one isolated −1.8σ point at 17 keV (neighbours positive — not a
break onset), the mid band a mixed-sign 130–160 keV cluster (interleaved +1.7σ/−2.0σ —
scatter, not a component), and the high band is data-starved. Bin 4 is a clean
CPL bin: a very hard rising slope (index ≈ −0.12) cutting off around
E_pk ≈ 245 keV, and nothing else. No systematic residual structure is judged REAL;
flags: none. (Arm-2 cross-check: the band-resolved Δstat table should show near-zero
contrasts in every band for every model — a large Δband anywhere would contradict this
note and be a finding about the figure or the stat.)
