## 1. Consensus story across bins

**Per-bin, this burst is a one-hump CPL burst; the hard tail exists only in the integrated spectrum.**

Every reviewed bin (0–8) converges on a single-peaked continuum with an exponential-like turnover and no residual structure judged real. The mechanism is remarkably uniform: whenever a model carries a free high-energy index, it either rails at β = −5 (bins 0, 3, 4; bin 1 via the MINOS excursion; bin 7's Band lower error reaches exactly −5) or drifts to cutoff-mimicry values β ≈ −4.2 to −4.7 with soft-open errors (bins 5, 6). DSBPL never finds a second break in any bin — it collapses onto SBPL/CPL with railed or degenerate parameters in all ten notes (see §2). CPL is the only simple model whose full parameter vector is clean in bins 0, 1, 3, 4, 6, 7; bins 5 and 8 are four-way statistical ties in which Band wins AIC by parsimony-scale margins (bin 8: Δ(−2lnL) = 4.6 over CPL, the only bin where CPL is measurably worse — its turnover pushed to ~55 keV leaves a sub-1σ positive drift at 27–55 keV).

**Spectral evolution is clean hard-to-soft.** Ep(curve) decays monotonically through the burst: ~515 keV (bin 0, pre-trigger) → ~473 (1) → ~286/246 (2) → ~267 (3) → ~245 (4) → ~150–170 (5) → ~132 (6) → ~88 (7) → ~37–55 keV (8). The low-energy index hardens into the pulse peak and softens after: α(CPL) ≈ −0.15 (0), −0.34 (1), −0.44 (2), −0.25 (3), **−0.12 (4), −0.04 (5)** — the α-max epoch, coincident with the brightest blocks (46.4σ, 40.5σ) — then −0.30 (6), −0.46 (7), −0.91 (8).

**Residual structures do not evolve — they never rise above noise per-bin.** Bins 2 and 3 each show a ≤2.3σ mid-band weave near 95–190 keV (opposite phase in the two bins); bins 4 and 5 show only isolated single-bin excursions; bin 6 shows a detector-split NB-positive drift; bins 7 and 8 are flat at sub-1σ. In every case the flexible models (free break, second break) fail to absorb the candidate feature, which each note takes as evidence of noise. Only T_INT produces structures flagged REAL: (i) all four detected B1 points (~300–700 keV) sit at positive residual under every simple model **with EAC_B1 railed at the +20% bound**, and (ii) CPL specifically converts that flat offset into a terminal positive run (~+1.3σ endpoints) — the data-harder-than-exponential signature that Band/SBPL β absorb. This is coherent with the T_INT winner being a +PL composite (SBPL+PL) while no individual bin demands one: either a persistent hard component below per-bin sensitivity, or the classic integration artifact in which summing an evolving CPL (Ep sliding 515 → 40 keV) manufactures a power-law-like wing. The synthesis cannot distinguish these; arm 2 and the composite-family notes must (§5, items 2–4).

## 2. Recurring systematic structures (flagged in 2+ bins)

1. **EAC rails at exactly 0.800 / 1.200 — 8 of 10 bins.** EAC_NB = 0.800 and EAC_B1 = 1.200 simultaneously in bins 0, 1, 3, 8 and T_INT (NB ≈ 0.802); EAC_B1 = 1.2 alone in bin 5 (CPL) and bin 6 (Band/CPL/DSBPL, 1.050 in SBPL); **bin 7 inverts the pattern — BOTH constants rail at 0.800** (fit wants nb *and* b1 pushed down ≥20% vs na). Only bins 2 (0.93/1.17) and 4 (0.805/1.15–1.17) are unrailed — the two brightest early blocks. Identical across models within each bin, so it cannot bias model selection, but it is a real, recurring cross-detector normalization tension: the fits systematically want NB suppressed and B1 boosted beyond the allowed ±20%, saturating hardest in the faint bins. The T_INT B1 underprediction (§1) is partly this tension expressed in residual space.
2. **β driven to the −5 rail / cutoff mimicry — 7 bins** (0, 1, 3, 4 hard rails; 5, 6, 7 steep-and-open). The single most consistent model-level signature in the burst: no bin measures a high-energy power law.
3. **DSBPL degeneracy — all 10 bins.** XB railed at the 5-MeV cap (0), β rail + AIC loss (1), α1 = α2 with XB error spanning to 5000 keV (2), concave-up (hardening) break opposite the Ravasio sense (3), MINOS failure + inverted slope hierarchy (4), α2 railed at +0.5 chasing a single 23-keV noise bin (5), α1–xb–K degeneracy with +1200% K error (6), XB spanning the full [10, 5000] keV domain (7), α1 = α2 to the third decimal with xb railed at both edges (8), α2 pinned at −2 with XP railed at the 30-keV floor and inverted break ordering (T_INT). **There is no bin in which the two-break model is even formulatable**, consistent with the lower-limit doctrine: this burst contributes zero two-break detections, not evidence against breaks.
4. **Mid-band ~90–190 keV wiggle locus — bins 2, 3 (and echoes in 4, T_INT).** Bin 2: −2σ at ~95 keV then +1–1.6σ at 110–150 keV; bin 3: +2σ at ~100, −2.3σ at ~150, +0.8 at ~190 — **opposite phase at the same energies**, which itself argues noise; bin 4 has an interleaved ±(1.7–2.0)σ cluster at 130–160 keV; T_INT an isolated −3σ NA bin at ~90 keV. Recurring energy locus, incoherent sign → most plausibly noise or something instrumental near ~100 keV (NaI), not a spectral feature; no model absorbs it anywhere. Quantitative check → §5 item 5.
5. **NB-vs-NA detector split, NB positive — bins 2 and 6.** Bin 2: the lone detected NB point (~110 keV) reads +1.5σ in every panel; bin 6: NB points systematically +1 to +2σ at 95–180 keV while NA straddles zero — flagged as cross-calibration, not spectrum, in both notes. Same detector, same sign, same energy range as item 4's locus; and the same detector whose EAC rails low elsewhere. One coherent instrumental story (NB normalization/response) could underlie items 1, 4, 5 — worth a dedicated look.
6. **Mild positive B1 tendency where BGO detects at all — bins 2, 3, (4), T_INT.** Sub-1σ per bin (B1 points +0.4 to +0.9σ in bin 2; +0.8/~0 in bin 3; +0.6/−0.3 in bin 4) but uniformly non-negative, culminating in the T_INT REAL flag. Direction-consistent with the EAC_B1 = 1.2 rails.
7. **Atlas blindness — bins 0 and 1.** All panels "NO DETECTED CHANNELS" despite OK fits at 5.5σ and 9.7σ. The bin-1 note identifies the likely cause: folded diagnostics use *current, unbound* inputs (fit-sidecar omission) while the working tree now holds freshly downloaded v01 nb files (`data/bn081125496/glg_cspec_nb_bn081125496_v01.pha`, `glg_tte_nb_bn081125496_v01.fit.gz`, `glg_cspec_nb_bn081125496_v00.rsp2`) — a fit-time vs render-time version mismatch. **Blocking defect for the vision arm on those bins; regenerate the atlas.** This is the panel–engine fidelity item already OPEN in the whole-project audit.

## 3. Contradictions (between bins, or note vs fit-table winner)

1. **Bin 2: note vs winner.** The note's verdict is "no simple-model failure motivates a composite from residuals in this bin" — yet the engine winner for bin 2 is **Band+PL**. The note saw the +PL only through the unfold convention, never as a residual demand. Either the +PL gain lives in a band the panels cannot express (BGO upper-limit region), or it is a statistical preference without localized residual support. Arm 2 must localize it (§5 item 3).
2. **Bin 6: note vs winner (inferred).** Same shape: the note concludes "CPL fully describes the bin; no composite is motivated," attributing the only structure (NB +1–2σ drift at 95–180 keV) to cross-calibration — yet by elimination the engine winner is **SBPL+PL**. If the +PL is feeding on the NB drift, the "instrumental" reading and the winner story directly conflict (§5 item 4). First, confirm the winner mapping itself.
3. **T_INT vs every per-bin note.** T_INT flags a REAL hard-tail/underpredicted high band (winner SBPL+PL); no individual bin shows it. Not a logical contradiction (sensitivity + integration artifact both explain it) but it is the burst's central unresolved tension, and it decides whether the composite winners are physics or bookkeeping.
4. **Bin 2 vs bin 3 weave phase.** Opposite sign patterns over the same 95–190 keV range in adjacent bins — a note-vs-note inconsistency that supports the noise reading both notes independently reached.
5. **Bin 8: tie verdict vs Band win.** The note calls the bin a four-way tie and judges CPL's 27–55 keV drift "not real," while the table separates Band from CPL by Δ(−2lnL) = 4.6 (ΔAIC ≈ 2.6). Mild, but it is the one bin where the reviewer's noise call and a non-trivial likelihood gap coexist.
6. **Table self-consistency defects (not spectral):** bin 0's BOUND_CAPPED column is empty ("--") despite β, XB, and EAC rails — the rail diagnostic did not fire; bin 6 SBPL suffered a MINOS failure with an implausibly tight COVARIANCE_MC K error (±2.8% vs CPL's ±25%) and a −2lnL *above* CPL's despite an extra parameter (inferior basin); bins 6 and 8 carry symmetric errors grossly inconsistent with their MINOS intervals (α1 in both). Any downstream consumer of the ECSV must not trust symmetric errors or the BOUND_CAPPED flag for this burst.

## 4. Implications for the frozen predictions

**P-A (no significant BB): SUPPORTED by the review.** No bin, at any epoch, shows the BB grammar signature (two-detector up–down mode / shoulder on the Ep rise). The only BB-adjacent candidate — bin 2's ~95→150 keV weave — is ≤2σ, single-point-driven, unabsorbed by any flexible model, and judged noise; its phase inverts in bin 3. The stored composite LRTs quoted in the notes are all null (Band+BB vs Band = 2.42 and CPL+BB vs CPL = 0.70 in bin 1; CPL+BB vs CPL = 0.32 in bin 4). Nothing in ten notes threatens P-A. Quantitative closure belongs to arm 2 (§5 item 9).

**Yu+2019 break-at-α-max: NOT SUPPORTED in this burst.** The α-max epoch is unambiguous — bins 4–5 (α ≈ −0.12 and −0.04, the brightest blocks, 2.1–3.7 s). Precisely there, the free-break machinery finds nothing: bin 4's DSBPL fails MINOS with an inverted slope hierarchy and its SBPL break (320 keV) sits *at the peak*, not below it; bin 5's DSBPL parks XB ≈ 23 keV on a single +2.5σ noise bin, rails α2 at +0.5, goes INVALID, and the noise bin survives in the strip untouched — the definitive anti-break demonstration. No low-energy residual weave exists in either bin. Caveats: (i) the detected band floors at ~13 keV, so a break below ~15–20 keV is untestable; (ii) the hard α values themselves (−0.12, −0.04, nominally above the synchrotron slow-cooling line) are the interesting fact these bins do establish; (iii) this is a per-burst null, not a refutation of the population claim. Arm 2 check → §5 item 10.
