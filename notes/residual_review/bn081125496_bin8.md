## Scene (what the panels actually show)

The displayed energy domain is narrow: ~10–70 keV (detected non-upper-limit channels plus a
0.08-dex pad). Detected points: five NA (green) channels near ~14, 18, 22, 27 and ~55 keV, and
one NB (orange) point at the ~10 keV left edge. Everything else — the remaining NB channels, the
~33 keV NA channel, and **all** B1/BGO channels — is 2σ upper limits sitting above every model
curve. There is no detected high-band (NaI >300 keV or BGO) data in this bin at all, so nothing
can be said about the high range for any model beyond "unconstrained". The residual strips carry
only the handful of detected NA points.

## BAND

1. **Verdict: good.** Residuals flat: all five points within roughly ±0.5σ (14–27 keV points at
   ~0, the ~55 keV point mildly positive, ≲+0.5σ). No run, no up–down mode, no terminal trend.
2. **Parameters.** Ep = 37.0 −6.4/+11.4 keV — genuinely constrained. alpha = +0.21 −0.92/+1.12 —
   effectively unconstrained (spans ~−0.7 to +1.3); beta = −2.31 −0.32/+0.19, set mostly by the
   ~55 keV point plus the upper-limit ceiling. K positive error is huge (+7.9 on 0.55),
   reflecting the alpha–K degeneracy. MINOS OK, VALID. Errors are large for a 15.5σ block
   because the detected band is only ~10–70 keV with ~6 points. **EAC railed:** EAC_NB = 0.800,
   EAC_B1 = 1.200 — both exactly at the correction bounds (see cross-model note below).
3. **Systematic structure:** none. Low (<30 keV NaI): clean. Mid (30–300 keV): one detected
   point + limits, all consistent. High: no detected data — cannot assess.
4. **Better/worse range:** low band (NA) is the best-measured and is well modeled; mid is
   consistent but data-poor; high is unconstrained (not "well modeled" — simply untested).
5. **Suggests:** nothing further. A single smoothly curved continuum with Ep ≈ 37 keV describes
   every detected channel; no composite hypothesis is motivated by this bin.

## CPL

1. **Verdict: good (adequate at worst).** The only quasi-structure among the four: a faint
   upward tilt — first point ~−0.3σ at 14 keV rising to ~+0.4σ at 27 keV and ~+0.3σ at 55 keV.
   Every point is sub-1σ; at this significance I do not judge this a real run, but it is the one
   residual pattern that differs from the other three models.
2. **Parameters.** index = −0.91 −0.37/+0.41; xc = 50.5 −17.1/+37.5 keV (Epk_curve ≈ 55 keV) —
   large but sane errors, nothing railed (EAC excepted, as for all models). MINOS OK, VALID.
   −2lnL = 2646.3, i.e. 4.6 worse than Band with one fewer parameter — consistent with the mild
   tilt: the exponential turnover pushed to ~55 keV leaves the 27–55 keV points slightly above
   the curve.
3. **Systematic structure:** the sub-1σ low-to-high positive drift noted above; onset ~20 keV if
   taken literally, but amplitude is below anything the grammar treats as a break signature.
   No terminal negative run (and no BGO data to host one).
4. **Better/worse range:** low band well modeled; mid band very slightly under-predicted
   (the +0.3…+0.4σ points); high untested.
5. **Suggests:** if the tilt were real it would say the curvature belongs at lower energy /
   softer than exponential — i.e. the Band/SBPL-style break the other models supply. Test is the
   nested LRT vs Band; here the −2lnL gap (4.6) is at the boundary of interesting and the
   residuals do not demand it. No evidence for a cutoff beyond the band.

## SBPL

1. **Verdict: good.** Residual strip indistinguishable from Band's: flat within ±0.5σ, last
   point mildly positive. No structure.
2. **Parameters.** Ebreak = 19.7 −9.2/+27.8 keV — spans nearly the whole detected band;
   alpha = +0.41 −1.29/+1.09, and the positive MINOS error hits the parameter bound exactly
   (0.4094 + 1.0906 = +1.500) — **alpha upper error is bound-limited**; beta = −2.62 −0.70/+0.35;
   K well determined (0.00516 ± 0.0007). MINOS OK, VALID. The fit is fine; the break location
   and low slope are just barely informed by the data.
3. **Systematic structure:** none in any band.
4. **Better/worse range:** low and mid equally well modeled; high untested.
5. **Suggests:** nothing beyond a single smooth break; no additional component indicated.

## DSBPL

1. **Verdict: good in residual space, but the model is degenerate here.** Residuals flat
   (third point a hair negative, last a hair positive, all ≪1σ) — the extra break buys no
   visible residual improvement over SBPL/Band.
2. **Parameters — the red flags of this bin.** alpha1 = −0.5402 and alpha2 = −0.5381 are equal
   to the third decimal: the two low-energy slopes have converged, so the first break does
   nothing. Consistently, xb = 19.1 keV has MINOS errors railed at **both** domain edges
   (19.116 − 9.116 = 10.000 keV; 19.116 + 4980.884 = 5000.000 keV) — xb is completely
   unconstrained across its entire allowed range. alpha1's negative MINOS error also rails
   (−0.540 − 1.960 = −2.500 exactly), and the tiny symmetric ALPHA1_ERR (0.012) is inconsistent
   with the huge MINOS asymmetry — the symmetric error is not to be trusted. xp = 38.5
   −6.0/+7.8 keV and beta = −2.42 −0.44/+0.24 are fine. MINOS_OK/VALID are set, but the fit has
   collapsed to an SBPL with two wasted parameters.
3. **Systematic structure:** none.
4. **Better/worse range:** low and mid well modeled; high untested.
5. **Suggests:** there is **no low-energy break** in this bin — DSBPL degenerates to a
   single-break shape. Any two-break census must not count bin 8 on VALID alone; the xb rail and
   alpha1≈alpha2 mark it as OUT-OF-FAMILY for the two-break hypothesis here.

## Comparative paragraph

All four simple continua describe bin 8 equally well: the residual strips are flat at the
±0.5σ level over the only band with detected data (~10–70 keV, essentially the NA detector plus
one NB point), and the models differ visibly only where there is nothing to test them —
their turnovers land at 37 (Band), 43 (SBPL), 55 (CPL) and 39 keV (DSBPL), all under upper
limits, all curves dotted beyond ~30 keV. The single point of differentiation is CPL, whose
exponential turnover pushed to ~55 keV produces a faint (sub-1σ) positive drift in the 27–55 keV
residuals and a −2lnL 4.6 worse than Band — i.e. the other three "absorb" that mild mid-band
tension by curving earlier; I judge the drift not real at this significance. DSBPL adds nothing:
its two low slopes converge and its first break is unconstrained edge-to-edge, so it is an SBPL
in costume. The data are featureless everywhere the models agree, and the four fits are a
statistical tie (−2lnL spread 4.6; ΔAIC spread 4.2, consulted after pass 1). One cross-model
systematic worth recording: **every model rails both effective-area constants at their bounds**
(EAC_NB = 0.800, EAC_B1 = 1.200 to machine precision) — with NB nearly all upper limits and B1
entirely so, the EACs are data-starved and drift to the corners; they are not measurements in
this bin.
