   within ±1 sigma nearly everywhere; the only structure is an up–down–up oscillation in
   the NaI mid band (+2 sigma near ~100 keV, −2.3 sigma near ~150 keV, back to +0.8 sigma
   at ~180–200 keV). No monotonic run at either end; B1 points sit at +0.8 and ~0 sigma.
2. **Parameters:** alpha = −0.254 (−0.126/+0.136) — sane, tight for a 26.5-sigma block;
   Ep = 268 (−20/+24) keV — well constrained (~9%); **beta = −5.000 railed at the lower
   bound** (MINOS −2×10⁻⁶/+1.67 — the fit is pinned, the positive error just measures the
   walk back off the rail); K = 0.124 ± 0.018. The rail is why the panel carries INVALID.
   EAC: nb = 0.800, b1 = 1.200 — **both exactly at their bounds** (see comparative note).
3. **Systematic structure:** low (<~30–50 keV): none — first NaI point ~0, one +1.7 sigma
   point near 45 keV with no neighbors following it. Mid (~30–300): the weave named above,
   2 of ~16 points beyond ±2 sigma. High (>300 keV / BGO): no terminal run in B1; nb gives
   only upper limits, all lying above the curve (consistent, uninformative).
4. **Better/worse ranges:** low band well modeled (na); mid band carries all the structure
   (na); high band well modeled but data-starved (b1 two points; nb nothing).
5. **Suggests:** the beta rail says the data demand no high-energy power law — the
   CPL family suffices; nothing in the residuals asks for more. The mid-band weave, if
   taken seriously, would point at low-energy-break/SBPL curvature — but see the
   comparative paragraph: the more flexible models do not absorb it.

## Comparative paragraph

All four simple continua converge on the same one-hump, sharply-cut spectrum: with beta
railed at −5 in Band, SBPL, and DSBPL, every model is effectively the CPL (Ep ≈ 255–270
keV), and their count-space residual strips are point-for-point identical. Consequently
**no model absorbs any structure that another shows**: the only candidate feature — the
mid-band up–down–up weave in na (+2 sigma near 100 keV, −2.3 sigma near 150 keV, positive
again by 190 keV) — appears at the same amplitude under all four, including the models
with a free break placed right in that band. Two of ~16 points beyond ±2 sigma is within
noise expectation, the weave has no coherent monotonic run, and the flexible models'
failure to absorb it argues NOISE; I do not call it real. The data are featureless within
statistics at low energy (na < 50 keV) and at high energy (b1, only two detected points;
nb contributes nothing but upper limits). The one genuinely systematic, model-independent
finding is not spectral: **the effective-area constants sit exactly at their bounds for
every model (EAC_nb = 0.800, EAC_b1 = 1.200)** — a cross-detector normalization tension
(fit wants nb suppressed by >20% and b1 raised by >20%) that the bounds are silently
capping; worth an engine-side look (nb's blockage/geometry in this bin) rather than a
spectral hypothesis. Engine agreement (PASS 2): CPL is the AIC and BIC winner and the
only valid model; ΔAIC Band +2.1, SBPL +3.0, DSBPL +4.6 — consistent with everything
above.
