## Comparative paragraph

The four simple continua produce residual strips that are indistinguishable at reading
resolution: every model shows the same weak mid-band down–up weave (a single −2σ dip at
~95 keV followed by a +1–1.6σ cluster at 110–150 keV) and none absorbs it — adding a
break (SBPL), a second break (DSBPL), or exchanging cutoff for tail (CPL↔Band) leaves it
untouched, which is itself evidence the weave is single-point noise rather than a
continuum-shape failure; at ≤2σ among ~17 points I judge it NOT real. The models agree
the data are featureless below ~50 keV (upper limits) and above ~300 keV (one BGO point,
sub-1σ under all four; the >500 keV upper limits sit far above every curve). Where the
models genuinely differ — Band's β tail vs CPL's exponential vs SBPL's soft β vs DSBPL's
collapsed extra break — the residuals carry no signal; Pass 2 shows the four fits within
ΔAIC ≈ 2.5 of one another (a tie), with Band cheapest and DSBPL's second break degenerate
(α1=α2, XB unbounded). Verdict for the bin: a single-peaked continuum with Ep ≈ 240–300
keV describes the data; no simple-model failure motivates a composite from residuals in
this bin.
