## BAND

1. **Fit verdict: good.** Residual strip is scatter-dominated within ±2.5σ across the full band. No monotonic run longer than ~3 bins anywhere. The only excursion beyond ±2.5σ is a single isolated NA bin near ~90 keV at ≈ -3σ whose neighbours are normal (single-bin fluctuation, not a run).
2. **Parameters (sane, MINOS, VALID):** alpha = -0.489 (-0.056/+0.061), Ep = 177.3 (-9.9/+10.2) keV, beta = -2.647 (-0.266/+0.177), K = 0.0792 (-0.0065/+0.0078). Nothing railed; ~6% error on Ep and ~12% on alpha are appropriate for a full-burst (T_INT) integration — this is a high-S/N bin and the errors are correspondingly tight.
3. **Systematic structure:** weak low-energy weave — points near 10–13 keV mildly positive (+0.5–1σ), a shallow negative drift over ~15–30 keV (mostly -0.5 to -1σ, one NB bin ≈ -2σ near 28 keV), then a mildly positive cluster at ~40–60 keV (+0.5–2σ). Amplitude per point ≤~1σ except the two named bins — marginal, not compelling. Mid band (60–300 keV): featureless apart from the isolated -3σ NA bin near 90 keV. High band: all four detected B1 points sit at positive residual (~+0.3 to +1.2σ) — a consistent mild offset, not a growing run; the Band beta is soft enough to track them in slope.
4. **Better/worse ranges:** low well modeled for NA and NB (weave is marginal in both); mid well modeled in NA and NB; high (B1) adequately modeled in slope but uniformly slightly underpredicted in normalization.
5. **Suggests:** the uniform positive B1 offset with the B1 EAC railed at +1.2 points to residual high-band flux the single continuum cannot supply even with maximum cross-normalization — the hard-tail/+PL family is the named hypothesis (and is where the engine's winner lives). The low-energy weave, taken alone, would point at a low-energy break or a BB shoulder, but its amplitude here does not demand it.

## Comparative paragraph

All four panels share the same unfolded points, so the comparison lives entirely in the residual strips, and three of them — Band, SBPL, DSBPL — are point-for-point indistinguishable: a marginal low-energy weave (shallow 15–30 keV deficit bracketed by mild positives at ~10 and ~40–60 keV, all ≤~1σ per point except single -2σ NB and -3σ NA bins), a featureless mid band, and a uniform ~+0.5–1σ offset in the four detected B1 points. The one real discriminant is CPL: its exponential tail converts the flat B1 offset into a terminal positive run (~+1.3σ endpoints), the classic data-harder-than-cutoff signature, which Band's beta = -2.65 and SBPL's smooth break absorb completely — so the high band is where the family separates, and it separates in favor of power-law tails. DSBPL demonstrates the converse at low energy: given two free breaks it does NOT chase the 15–30 keV weave but collapses onto SBPL with railed parameters, which is the strongest available evidence that the weave is noise-level in this bin. The data are genuinely featureless in the mid band (60–300 keV) for every model — the -3σ NA bin near 90 keV is isolated in all four strips and I do not read it as structure. What no simple model removes is the B1 normalization offset, and PASS 2 shows every model pushing the B1 effective-area constant to its +20% rail (and NB to its 0.8 rail) trying: the residual high-band flux is a property of the bin, not of any one continuum, and it is the natural opening for the +PL composite family.

## Flags (structures judged REAL)

1. **B1 high-band underprediction, all models:** all four detected B1 points (~300–700 keV) sit at positive residual in every simple model while EAC_B1 is railed at the 1.2 upper bound (1.19999... in all four fits; EAC_NB simultaneously at ~0.802 ≈ the lower rail) — the model family underpredicts the high band even at maximum allowed cross-normalization. Coherent with the engine winner being a +PL composite; also worth a calibration sanity check since it expresses partly as an inter-detector normalization tension.
2. **CPL terminal positive B1 run:** real structure relative to CPL specifically — the spectrum retains a hard tail above ~300 keV that an exponential cutoff cannot carry; the Band/SBPL beta family absorbs it.
3. **DSBPL rail/degeneracy (fit pathology, not spectral structure):** ALPHA2 pinned at -2, XP railed at the 30 keV floor with an unconstrained upper MINOS error and inverted break ordering; ΔlnL over SBPL = 0.23 for 2 dof. INVALID label verified correct.
