## PASS 1 — residual structure seen in the images (recorded before consulting the table)

Common to all four panels: NA detected 13 to ~200 keV, NB detected ~80–140 keV plus scattered
upper limits; the nuFnu spectrum rises steeply (roughly slope ~1.3 in log-log) to a peak of
~1000 keV^2 s^-1 cm^-2 keV^-1 at ~100–150 keV, with the turnover only marginally sampled at
the top of the band. The residual strips are dominated by three isolated features, identical
in every panel:

1. a single NA point at ~22–25 keV at **+2.5 to +2.7 sigma**, with both neighbours at
   |resid| < 1 sigma — an isolated single-bin excess, not a run; the NB upper limit at the
   same energy sits above the model and does not corroborate it;
2. a single NA point at ~160–190 keV at **−2.4 to −2.6 sigma**, at the terminal edge of the
   detected domain, preceded by a mildly positive cluster (~+0.3 to +1.5 sigma) over
   ~90–140 keV — a weak up-then-down at the top of the band, carried by one point;
3. a −1.5 to −1.7 sigma dip at ~80 keV.

No monotonic runs, no coherent weave, no detector-vs-detector offset visible. The four
strips are near-identical: BAND and SBPL are visually indistinguishable from each other, as
are CPL and DSBPL; inter-model differences are confined to the last one or two points
(~160–200 keV) at the ≲0.5 sigma level, below my confident visual resolution.

## BAND

1. **Fit verdict: good.** Residuals flat across the detected band; the only excursions are
   the three isolated features listed above (one bin each), with ~30 plotted bins — a +2.7
   and a −2.4 among them is compatible with noise. No runs.
2. **Parameters (PASS 2):** alpha = +0.085 ± 0.17, Ep = 152.6 (−12.3/+14.6) keV,
   beta = −2.84 (−0.85/+0.38), K = 0.315 (−0.065/+0.088). VALID=True, STATUS=OK, MINOS
   errors. Nothing railed. alpha is unusually hard (positive) but consistent with ~0 at
   1 sigma; the asymptotic low-energy slope is never reached in-band, so the large alpha
   error despite S = 40.5 sigma is expected. beta's fat lower error (−0.85) reflects the
   absent high band — beta is only weakly constrained by NB upper limits. Ep at 9% is
   healthy for the block significance. EAC: nb 0.811, b1 1.071 — unremarkable.
3. **Systematic structure:** none judged real. Low: the isolated +2.7 sigma NA bin at
   ~24 keV (single-bin, single-detector). Mid: clean except the −1.7 dip at 80 keV. High
   (top of NaI, ~160 keV): single terminal −2.4 sigma point after a mildly positive
   90–140 keV cluster; one point does not make a terminal run.
4. **Better/worse ranges:** mid (30–300 keV) best modeled in both NA and NB; low (<30 keV)
   good except the one 24 keV bin (NA only); high band not assessable (upper limits only).
5. **What it suggests:** nothing beyond Band. If the terminal negative point were a run it
   would point at the cutoff family (test = LRT of CPL-like curvature vs Band), but CPL
   shows the identical point (see comparative paragraph), so the data do not ask for it.

## CPL

1. **Fit verdict: good.** Residual strip statistically indistinguishable from BAND's: same
   three isolated features, no runs. The terminal −2.5 sigma point persists despite the
   exponential cutoff — CPL does not absorb it either.
2. **Parameters (PASS 2):** index = −0.043 ± 0.11, XC = 85.6 (−7.9/+9.5) keV → curve Ep
   ≈ 167 keV, K = 0.313 (−0.10/+0.14). VALID=True, MINOS OK. Nothing railed among the
   spectral parameters; errors tight and symmetric, appropriate for 40.5 sigma. One
   nuisance-level rail: **EAC_B1 = 1.19999988 — pinned at the 1.2 bound**; since B1 has no
   detected counts in the fitted display domain this is cosmetically harmless here, but it
   is a railed constant and worth knowing.
3. **Systematic structure:** same inventory as BAND — isolated +2.5 at ~22 keV (low),
   −1.6 dip at 80 keV (mid), terminal −2.5 near ~185 keV. No runs.
4. **Better/worse ranges:** identical statement to BAND — mid best, low fine bar the one
   bin, high unassessable.
5. **What it suggests:** CPL is fully sufficient; Ep(curve) 167 keV vs Band's 154 keV and
   ΔAIC ≈ 0.4 mean beta is doing no measurable work — the high-energy photon deficit
   (upper limits only) cannot distinguish Band beta = −2.8 from an exponential tail.

## SBPL

1. **Fit verdict: good.** Residual strip visually identical to BAND's; same three isolated
   features; no structure of its own and none absorbed.
2. **Parameters (PASS 2):** alpha = −0.16 (−0.16/+0.21), Ebreak = 174.7 (−53.5/+55.1) keV,
   beta = −4.24 (−0.76/+0.94), K = 0.0805 ± 0.003. VALID=True, MINOS OK, nothing railed.
   Ebreak carries a ~30% error and beta is both steep and loose because the break sits at
   the very top of the detected range — the post-break slope is sampled by essentially
   nothing but upper limits. beta = −4.2 is SBPL's way of imitating a quasi-exponential
   turnover; it should not be read as a measured high-energy photon index.
3. **Systematic structure:** same three isolated features as BAND (low +2.7 at ~24 keV,
   mid −1.7 at 80 keV, terminal −2.4 at ~160 keV). No runs.
4. **Better/worse ranges:** mid best; low fine except the single bin; high unassessable.
5. **What it suggests:** with the break degenerate with the peak and beta unphysically
   steep, SBPL adds nothing over CPL/Band here; no follow-up hypothesis is generated.

## DSBPL

1. **Fit verdict: adequate in residuals, poor as an estimate.** The residual strip is as
   flat as the other three — visually indistinguishable from CPL's, including the same
   +2.3–2.5 sigma bin at ~22 keV, which the extra break does NOT remove — but the
   parameter vector is degenerate (below), so the model is not delivering a meaningful
   two-break description.
2. **Parameters (PASS 2):** alpha1 = −0.82 (−0.43/+0.24), XB = 22.9 (−11.8/+43.8) keV,
   **alpha2 = +0.49999 railed at the +0.5 bound (pos. err 5.6e-06, neg. err −2.66)**,
   XP = 162.0 (−18.8/+23.6) keV, beta = −3.11 (−0.41/+0.38), **K = 3.90 (−2.50/+7.05) —
   error larger than the value**. STATUS=OK but **VALID=False**. XB's error spans a factor
   ~3 and lands exactly where the single 23 keV noise bin sits; the engine's LRT of DSBPL
   vs SBPL = 2.0, i.e. the second break is statistically nothing.
3. **Systematic structure:** same three isolated features as the others; notably the low
   break at XB ≈ 23 keV chases the single-bin spike yet the spike survives in the strip —
   consistent with it being one high bin, not a spectral break.
4. **Better/worse ranges:** identical to the others — mid best, low fine except the one
   bin, high unassessable.
5. **What it suggests:** the data contain no low-energy break for DSBPL to find in this
   bin; the railed alpha2 and no-op XB are the classic over-parameterization signature.
   No follow-up needed; the two-break hypothesis is not supported in bin 5.

## Comparative paragraph (all four models)

This bin is a statistical four-way tie in which no model absorbs any structure another one
shows: the ΔAIC ladder burned into the panels (Band +0.0, CPL +0.4, SBPL +0.8, DSBPL +2.8
INVALID; table: BEST_AIC = Band, BEST_BIC = CPL) matches what the eye sees — four
near-identical residual strips whose only excursions are three isolated single-bin features
(+2.5–2.7 sigma at ~24 keV in NA, −1.7 at 80 keV, −2.4–2.6 at the ~160–190 keV terminal
point). The 24 keV excess is single-detector and single-bin, is not corroborated by NB, and
survives unchanged even when DSBPL parks a break right on it, so I judge it noise, not a
low-energy break or edge feature. The terminal negative point would grammar-parse as the
onset of a cutoff, but it persists identically under CPL's exponential tail, which is the
model that would absorb a real cutoff — one point, no run, not real. Between 30 and
300 keV all four models agree the data are featureless: a single, hard-rising
(alpha ~ 0), well-measured peaked continuum with Ep(curve) ≈ 150–170 keV. Band's beta,
SBPL's beta, and CPL's tail are interchangeable because the high band holds only upper
limits; DSBPL is the same curve wearing two extra degenerate parameters (alpha2 railed at
+0.5, VALID=False). Verdict for the bin: the simple-continuum menu is closed at
Band ~ CPL; nothing in the residuals motivates a composite.
