## Model selection — a REAL difference, correctly attributed
Best model at T_INT is **SBPL** by both AIC and BIC, not Band. This is **not** a contradiction:
the published analyses fit **only Band** (GBM's catalog uses a fixed 4-model menu; Konus quote
the Band model). Our 24-model menu is a strict superset, so a different winner is expected —
the same note already recorded for bn081224887. Band remains VALID here and its parameters
agree; the data simply prefer a smoothly-broken power law.

## Thermal component: NOT detected
`LRT(Band+BB vs Band) = 1.6` at T_INT — far below the 9.2 (3σ, 2 dof) admission threshold.
Per-block, only **blk 4 (4.99–5.95 s)** reaches LRT = 7.5 with kT = 9.4 keV, still sub-threshold
and selected as Band+BB by AIC alone. **No photospheric claim is supported for this burst.**

## Spectral evolution (12 blocks)
Ep: 229 → 290 → 136 → 219 → 230 → 254 → **330** (6.1–7.1 s) → 326 → 219 → 140 → 100 → 115 keV
— i.e. a rise to ~330 keV around the pulse peak, then a monotonic soft decline through the tail.
α hardens late (−0.20 at 14–20 s, +0.02 at 20–27 s).

**Line-of-death check (honest):** seven blocks have α above the synchrotron −2/3 limit, but
**none by more than 1.2σ** — the late blocks carry huge errors (±0.52, ±0.81). **No significant
violation; do not claim one.**

## Flags raised by this burst
- **F-2** (HIGH): b0's approved post window overlapped the source by 3.84 s — a catalog-wide
  sweep then found **21 such rows across 13+ bursts** (worst: bn090530760 b0 by 136 s).
- **F-4 / L5-1**: 27b discarded the approved t2 = 36.0 and substituted 27.35 s; 27–36 s emission
  (6.7–8.2σ per 5 s stacked) never became spectral bins.
- **F-5**: post window was on the burst tail (4.5σ in 41–60 s); correcting it changed net source
  counts by +2.7 % (n0) / +2.3 % (n1).
- **F-6**: `HAS_LAT=True` but LAT was in the SAA until T0+110 s — no prompt LAT/LLE coverage.
- **F-7**: GCN calls it "multi-peaked"; a control-calibrated peak count shows it is
  indistinguishable from the canonical single-pulse control. **Membership upheld.**

## Step 8 addendum — T_INT panel (added after `--bin tint` patch)

`figures/bn200524211_nuFnu_TINT_allmodels.png` — all 24 models on the interval the P3 diff quotes.

**Independent confirmation of the winner.** The engine and the `scripts/41` refit **agree at
T_INT**: both pick **SBPL** (engine 7009.2, panel 7009), and 10 of 12 models agree within ±1 AIC.
This bounds F-10: the tools diverge in low-count bins and on pathological composites, not on
informative intervals. (Extreme case: CPL+BB engine 7019.0 vs panel 8014 — a 995 AIC gap where
the panel's refit fell into a catastrophic minimum, its residuals showing a coherent −4→+4 wave.)

**⚠ Qualifier on the headline "best model = SBPL".** ΔAIC(Band − SBPL) = **2.4** → evidence
ratio **3.4 : 1**, well below the L16 "strong" bar (ΔAIC ≥ 6 → 20 : 1). **SBPL is only marginally
preferred over Band.** This does not weaken the literature comparison — it strengthens it: Band
remains a fully acceptable description of these data, which is precisely the model GBM and Konus
fitted, and our Band parameters agree with both to <1.1σ. The correct statement is *"the data
mildly prefer SBPL, and are equally well described by Band"* — not *"the burst is SBPL, not Band"*.
