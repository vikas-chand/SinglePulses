| **BAND, BANDBB, BANDCUT** | 2–3/16 | 0/16 | **0/16** | −8.96 … −9.71 |

SBPL α: nothing at +1.5 any more; **SBPLBBPL α = +2.5 in 3/16**, SBPLBBCPL in 2/16.

**Reading.** For the *pure Band-type* families the wider bound genuinely freed β — it now
lands at −8.9…−9.4 instead of pinned. For the *SBPL+BB* families it did not: β simply runs
to whatever floor exists. That is the classic β-degeneracy of a spectrum with too few
photons above E_p to constrain a second slope. **β = −10 is no more measured than β = −5.**

**Consequence:** the argmin flipped in **9 of 16** cells between the two bounds settings —
but with 15/16 ties in both tables, those flips are reshuffling *inside* a tie, not a change
of preference.
