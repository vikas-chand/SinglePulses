## ⭐ DEDICATED PAPER — Qin, Jiang & Wang 2021, RAA 21, 72 (`2021RAA....21...72Q`)
*"GRB 130310A: very high peak energy and thermal emission"* — PDF fetched from IOP (open access;
no arXiv version). **This is the single most relevant paper we have hit so far.** Its claims:
- **Band+BB time-resolved**: a **sub-dominant thermal component** early (slice T0+[4.03–4.14] s)
  with **kT ~ 5–7 keV**, *"which can be interpreted as photosphere emission."*
- **Precursor** fits a pure BB with **kT ~ 45 keV** — HOTTER than the main burst ⇒ they read a
  **thermal → non-thermal transition**, i.e. *"a change in jet composition from a fireball to a
  Poynting-flux-dominated jet."*
- **Extreme Ep**: first time bin **Ep ≈ 8.5–5.2 MeV (Band+BB)** / **11.1–7.4 MeV (Band)**, dropping
  to ~1 MeV afterwards; **hard-to-soft** overall.
- Their own difficulty, verbatim: *"The interpretation of the high peak energy Ep within the
  photosphere and internal shock model is difficult … the Band component must invoke a
  **non-thermal origin in the optically thin region**."* They float **ICMART** with low σ, and note
  photosphere+internal-shock works only with γ_e ~ 60, ε_e ~ 0.06.

### Why this burst is a decisive test case for our open questions
1. **#42 Arm A vs Arm B, head-on.** Qin's kT~5–7 keV sub-dominant BB is exactly the
   *"low-energy bump typically interpreted to be of photospheric origin"* that **RGB2024
   (`2024MNRAS.528L..45R`) says is a weaker FORWARD SHOCK.** Same data, two mechanisms — and here
   a published photospheric claim to test against.
2. **Our blk0 = [+4.089, +4.099] s (8 ms) sits INSIDE Qin's thermal slice T0+[4.03–4.14] s.**
   Direct, bin-level comparison is possible.
3. **Ep ~ 8.5–11 MeV tests our L9 bound fix.** Our Band `xp.bounds = (30, 5.0e4)` keV = up to
   50 MeV ✔ so 8–11 MeV is reachable (this is exactly the 5-MeV-cap failure that made 110721A
   falsely INCONCLUSIVE — L9). **Watch for `BOUND_CAPPED` anyway**, and note `DSBPL xb.bounds`
   still caps at 5000 keV.
4. Feeds **#34** (is a high-E cutoff genuine or count-limited) — a burst with Ep ~ MeV and LLE is
   the ideal case.
