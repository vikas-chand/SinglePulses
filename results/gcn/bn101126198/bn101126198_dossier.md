## Cross-paper consensus + tensions (Rossi+2026 §4.1 consensus rule applied)
- **Ep(t) is TRACKING, and three independent analyses agree** on the shape:
  Lu+2018 Band Ep rises 143 → **180 keV (11.85–13.07 s)** → 100 keV by 40 s and is
  classified **T**; Yu+2019 CPL Ep rises 94.8 (0.04–6.64 s) → **205 keV (11.75–14.67 s,
  the S = 63 peak block)** → 128 keV by 20 s, classified **"flat to h.t.s."**
  Both put the Ep maximum at **t ≈ 12–14 s**, coincident with Lu's FRED peak
  tm = 12.70 s. **Consensus peak-block Ep ≈ 180–205 keV (CPL/Band frame split:
  Band Ep runs ~15–25% below CPL Ep in the same block — a model-frame offset, not a
  disagreement).** No paper calls this burst hard-to-soft; Shao makes no HIT claim
  for it. **A pure monotonic HTS track in our blind fit would contradict all of them.**
- **Low-energy index:** high-S blocks give α ∈ [−1.38, −0.81] (Yu CPL and Band) and
  [−1.37, −0.99] (Lu Band). **Nothing here violates the synchrotron "line of death"**
  (α > −2/3 never occurs in a constrained block) — unlike the bn100612726 case.
- **High-energy index:** β from −1.91 to −3.18 (Yu Band) and −2.00 to −2.97 (Lu),
  i.e. a well-behaved β ≈ −2 … −3, poorly bounded only in the faint bins. A BGO
  watch-item but no anomaly.
- **Thermal component: NO paper fits or claims a blackbody for this burst.**
  Yu+2019 fitted only CPL and Band; Lu+2018 only Band; nobody ran a BB or a hybrid.
  ⇒ Our archival union-rule BB blocks (see the P0 freeze) are **entirely ours to
  defend** — there is no literature prior to lean on, and equally no literature bias
  pushing us toward one.
- **Curvature:** Yu+2015's ⟨θ⟩ = 116° says the spectra are **too sharp for Maxwellian
  synchrotron** — an independent-method statement that *something* narrows the peak.
  Our BB/DSBPL search is testing the same physics with a different instrument;
  agreement or disagreement here is genuinely informative.
- **Lag:** τ̂₃₁ = 0.44 ± 0.16 s (Lu+2018, CCF between their bands 3 and 1) and
  Δt_HR = 1.749 ± 0.159 s (Shao+2017, hardness–intensity delay). **Different
  definitions — never diff one against the other.** You+2026's DTW lags for this
  same 50-burst sample are unfetched (see above).
- **Pulse shape:** Lu's FRED gives r = 2.62 (rise) vs d = 14.53 (decay) about
  tm = 12.70 s → a strongly **asymmetric FRED** (long tail). Shao's symmetric-Gaussian
  ω = 6.19 s is the fit You+2026 warns is biased for asymmetric pulses. Our Step-7
  asymmetry work should expect a FRED-like, tail-heavy pulse.
