## Instrument story
GBM triggered at 16:37:46.05 UT; a bright single pulse with weak extended emission.
The burst was IPN-triangulated (Konus, HEND, SPI-ACS, BAT out-of-FoV) to a 2.31 sq.
deg box — never localized to arcmin, hence no ground follow-up and no redshift.
Insight-HXMT/HE saw only the hard bright pulse (T90=5.48 s at >~200 keV), while
GBM/KW T90 (~58/66 s) includes the soft tail — a textbook energy-dependent-T90
spread, NOT an inconsistency (LiteratureHarvest Phase 4 frame rule).

**Window frame-note (D4):** our stamped source window is
[-1.401, +14.454] s (results/background_intervals.ecsv, APPROVED_BY=Vikas Chand,
human_gui, 2026-07-19T22:18:36Z; detectors n2,n5,b0). It covers the bright pulse
plus early tail — NOT the full ~58-66 s T90. Circular time-integrated spectra
(0..58 s GBM; 0..72.4 s KW) are therefore CROSS-FRAME to our fits; the KW
peak spectrum (T0..T0+6.912 s) is the closest external frame.

4. **Luo, Qi et al. 2020, JHEAp 27, 1 (2020JHEAp..27....1L; arXiv:2005.01367) —
   the ONLY refereed paper.** HXMT/CsI calibration paper; GRB 181212A is one of 10
   joint-fit sample GRBs. Table 4 joint Band fit (HXMT/CsI + Fermi/GBM 2 NaI + 1 BGO,
   time-integrated; direction theta,phi = 142,77 deg):
   **alpha = -1.40 +0.02/-0.03, beta = -2.96 +0.07/-0.18, Epeak = 105.0 +14.6/-9.1 keV**,

   **A_FN(NaI) = 6.40 +0.31/-0.55**, chi2/dof = 661/586. Fig 17(f) shows the joint fit
   (NG mode). Verbatim (p.14): *"The results show that the amplitudes obtained by all
   the instruments are consistent for all GRBs except GRB 181212A. For GRB 181212A,
   the HXMT/CsI amplitude is similar to Fermi/GBM BGO detector, but deviates
   significantly from Fermi/GBM NaI detector. It is worth noting that such an
   amplitude deviation still holds even if only the Fermi/GBM data are considered.
   This may suggest the possible shortages either in the adopted model or the
   understanding of the Fermi/GBM response."* And (p.16): *"In order to avoid the
   potential uncertainty described above, GRB 181212A is excluded from the sample."*
   -> a ~40% NaI-vs-BGO normalization tension published for THIS burst — directly
   relevant to our EAC cross-norms and to any low-energy-component claim (D5 class).
   Their fit interval for 181212A is not stated per-burst in the text we extracted
   (their §5 merges 18 CsI spectra; treat as burst-integrated).
