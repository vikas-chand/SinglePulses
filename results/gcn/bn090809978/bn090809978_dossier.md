## Instrument story
GBM triggered and localized (coarse, ~deg). LAT at 81° — outside the LAT FoV; no LLE.
No X-ray/optical follow-up was possible (GBM-only localization), so no afterglow and no z.
INTEGRAL IBIS/PICsIT (Earth-viewing archive survey, Bianchin+2011) independently detected
the burst at 23:28:17 UT, off-axis 53.7°: 3399±257 counts (260–1196 keV), T90 = 9±1 s,
fluence 1.63±0.02 e-6 erg/cm2 extrapolated 260–2600 keV (their Table 3; "F, K, IA" flags =
also seen by Fermi, Konus-Wind, SPI-ACS). PICsIT light curve: their Fig. 48.
