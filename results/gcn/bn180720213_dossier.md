# GCN dossier — bn180720213 = GRB 180720A (walkthrough tally #29)
**Step 0/0b, 2026-08-11.** Raw circulars: `results/gcn/bn180720213_gcn_raw.txt`.

## ⚠ SAME-DAY TRAP (T1) — the sibling is FAMOUS
Same UT day hosts **GRB 180720B** (Swift trigger 848890, 14:21 UT; bn180720598; z=0.654;
VHE H.E.S.S. burst) — and Swift *initially misnamed it "GRB 180720A"* (GCN 22973), corrected
in **GCN 22975**: *"The GRB name in GCN Circ. #22973 should be GRB 180720B because of an
earlier detection of GRB 180720A from AGILE/MCAL (GCN Circ. #22970)."* Every literature
"GRB 180720A" hit must be identity-checked by trigger/time — all three refereed ADS hits
turned out to be misnamed 180720B (see PUBLISHED VALUES below). ADS still lists GCN 22973
and 22984 under the "180720A" title; they are B-burst circulars.

## Identity card
**GRB 180720A** | GBM trigger **553755967 / 180720213** at **05:06:02.06 UT, 2018-07-20**
(verbatim GCN 22974; trigdat TRIGTIME MET 553755967.058 matches) | GBM ground position
RA, Dec = 127.0, −0.4 (J2000), ±1.0° stat (1σ) + core-plus-tail sys (GCN 22974; trigdat
RA_OBJ=129.02, DEC_OBJ=−13.25 is the onboard value) | **no redshift** (GBM+AGILE only; no
afterglow follow-up of the A burst in any circular) | LAT boresight angle 74° at trigger
(GCN 22974) — no LAT/LLE detection claimed | AGILE/MCAL detection (GCN 22970).
**n_circulars = 3** (22970 AGILE, 22974 GBM, 22975 naming correction). That is the complete
GCN record for the A burst; everything else in the 180720 archive is 180720B or 180720C.

## Per-circular quotes
- **GCN 22970** (Ursi et al., AGILE/MCAL, 07:09 UT): *"The AGILE Mini-CALorimeter (MCAL)
  detected a burst at T0 = 2018-07-20 05:06:03.29 +/- 0.01 s (UTC), lasting about ~ 3.6 s
  in the 0.4-100 MeV energy range. The light curve is composed of a single smooth peak and
  released a total of ~3000 counts at E> 400 keV in the detector, above an average
  background rate of 20 counts / 32 ms."*
- **GCN 22974** (Bissaldi, Fermi GBM, 14:39 UT): *"At 05:06:02.06 UT on 20 July 2018, the
  Fermi Gamma-Ray Burst Monitor triggered and located GRB 180720A (trigger 553755967 /
  180720213), which was also detected by the AGILE/MCAL (Ursi et al. 2018, GCN 22970)."*
  … *"The GBM light curve consists of single FRED-like peak with a duration (T90) of about
  9 s (50-300 keV)."* Spectral/fluence values quoted in full below.
- **GCN 22975** (Lien/Swift team, 14:48 UT): naming correction (quoted above).

## Redshift
**None.** No optical/X-ray follow-up of the A burst exists in the GCN record (the ~1°+sys
GBM-only localization was never tiled). Any z attached to "180720A" in the literature is
the B burst's z=0.654 leaking through the pre-correction name.

### Contaminated hits (identity-checked, NOT our burst — kept for the record)
- **Maistrello et al. 2024, A&A 684, L10** (`2024A&A...684L..10M`): Ep,i–Liso sample member
  is **180720B** (their table lists 180720B with z=0.654); "180720A" on pp. 5–6 of the
  arXiv PDF is an internal typo for the same B burst (the correlation requires z).
- **Moradi et al. 2021, PhRvD 104, 063043** (`2021PhRvD.104f3043M`): *"the X-ray afterglow
  in GRB 130427A, GRB 160509A, GRB 160625B, GRB 180720A and GRB 190114C"* — BdHN list;
  misnaming of 180720B (all listed bursts have z + GeV afterglows; ours has neither).
- **Wang et al. 2019, ApJ 874, 39** (`2019ApJ...874...39W`, already in corpus for
  bn180728728): *"We also predicted the SN appearance in … GRB 180720A (Ruffini et al.
  2018a)"* — that SN prediction was made for **180720B** (Ruffini GCN, verbatim: *"In GRB
  180720B, which has redshift z = 0.654 (GCN 22996), being a BdHN, a supernova is
  expected"*). Pre-correction-name leakage.

## BEYOND-PROMPT CONTEXT (future modules)
Nothing to defer **for this burst**: no afterglow, SN, cosmology, or multimessenger paper
exists for GRB 180720A itself. The rich multiwavelength record of 2018-07-20 (H.E.S.S. VHE
detection, SN prediction, Ep,i–Liso membership) all belongs to sibling GRB 180720B
(bn180720598), which is not in our sample holdings under this trigger. The literature's
naming confusion is itself the finding: three refereed papers say "180720A" and mean B.

## Why this burst matters to the campaign
A clean paperless FRED with a strong GBM circular anchor (Band 260 keV) and an independent
AGILE hard-band duration — the P3 diff will be circular-only, testing the pipeline's
behaviour when the literature offers no refereed frame at all.
