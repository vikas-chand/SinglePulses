## Instrument story

GBM triggered at 04:19:51 UT on a burst whose **main pulse runs ≈T0 → T0+30 s**. The
catalog duration analysis (NaI 0+1+3+4) returns T90 = 148 s **because it starts at
T0 − 119.3 s**: the catalog team found a **precursor at ~T0 − 120 s**, localized it,
confirmed it consistent with the burst position, and **included it in the duration
analysis** (footnote n). T50 = 15.4 s starting at T0+0.58 s describes the main pulse alone —
the ~10:1 T90/T50 ratio is the precursor's signature, not a long tail.

Seven instruments recorded the event (GBM, SPI-ACS, Konus-Wind, MESSENGER, Mars Odyssey,
RHESSI, Suzaku-WAM), and IPN triangulation shrank the GBM 3σ contour from 719 deg² to
0.212 deg² — a 3390× improvement — but 0.212 deg² is still far too coarse for optical
follow-up, so there is **no afterglow, no host, and no redshift**. No GCN circular was ever
issued for it.

**Window frame-note (D4 / T9).** Our stamped source window is
**[−7.338, +30.455] s** (`results/background_intervals.ecsv`, APPROVED_BY = Vikas Chand,
`human_gui`, 2026-07-19T22:18:36Z, `WINDOW_SOURCE = accepted_suggestion`; detectors
n0, n1, n3, n4, b0). It covers the **MAIN PULSE ONLY**. The precursor at T0−120 s is
outside it, and outside every background window except by ~17 s (n4's pre-window opens at
T0 − 102.99 s). **Every catalog duration/fluence number on this burst therefore has a
different component coverage than our fits**, and a T90 or fluence diffed against ours
without that label is a manufactured discrepancy.

## BEYOND-PROMPT CONTEXT (future modules)

Essentially empty for this burst, and that is itself the finding. There is **no afterglow
paper, no host study, no SN association, no cosmology use** — the IPN box (0.212 deg²) was
never small enough to trigger optical follow-up, and no redshift exists. The only
non-prompt-adjacent material is the multimessenger/IPN thread:

- **Hurley+2017** — cross-mission timing/localization (`module-future; mm`). A 7-instrument
  IPN detection is the raw material for any future joint-mission timing analysis.
- **Augusto+2012** (Auger/Tupi ground-level muon search) — **excluded as contamination**,
  but worth keeping on the mm shelf as a *method* example of ground-array GRB coincidence
  searches; its target was the MAXI J1836−194 image trigger, not this burst.

Nothing in the literature "wants" this burst's prompt spectrum to be anything in
particular — there is no photospheric claim, no synchrotron claim, no model advocacy to
disclose as a prior bias in P3. That makes it a clean null-streak baseline burst.
