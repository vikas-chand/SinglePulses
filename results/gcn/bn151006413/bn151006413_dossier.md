## 3. Redshift: **NONE**

Honest absence. Despite an arcsecond XRT position and an aggressive optical campaign
(Kanata/HOWPol at T+73 s, MITSuME Okayama at T+93 s, UVOT from T+36 s, OSN, Mondy,
MITSuME Akeno, MITSuME Ks-band), **no optical/NIR afterglow was ever detected** and no
spectroscopy was obtained. Deepest limits: R > 22.4 (Mondy, GCN 18416), I ≈ 21
(OSN, GCN 18411), white > 21.4 (UVOT, GCN 18405), Ks > 15.2 (GCN 18408).
The XRT excess column **N_H = 4.3–4.9 × 10²¹ cm⁻²** vs Galactic 7.7 × 10²⁰ cm⁻²
(7.1σ excess, GCN 18407) plus the optical non-detections make this a **dark burst** —
so no redshift-dependent quantity (E_iso, rest-frame Ep, Amati placement) is available
for this burst. *No circular anywhere in the file contains the string "z =".*
