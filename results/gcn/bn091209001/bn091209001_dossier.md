### Bordoloi, Mittal & Iyyani 2026 (ApJ 1003, 58; arXiv:2511.20310) — the ONLY refereed paper

Role of our burst: member of the **initial 41-burst sample** (catalog-selected bright GBM
bursts with quasi-thermal Band slopes, criteria α > −0.5 and −1.7 > β > −3.3), fit
time-resolved with Band; **NOT in the final 4-burst IC sample** (131014A, 200412B, 200829A,
230614C). Its exclusion is visible in its own rows: β rails at −5 in 4/5 bins (no
