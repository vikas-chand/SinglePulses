mismatch is not component coverage but **interval coverage**: the catalog fluence spectrum runs

photon indices, sign as printed; energy band **10–1000 keV** for fluence/flux (`flu_low`/`flu_high`),

the catalog spectra = **n0, n1, n9, b1** (`scat_detector_mask`) — **not the same set as ours** (n0, n1, b0).
