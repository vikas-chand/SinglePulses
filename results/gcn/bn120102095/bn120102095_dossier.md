### 2.1 The component story (T9 / D4 — this burst has structure OUTSIDE our window)

GCN 12799 (BAT), verbatim:
> *"The mask-weighted light curve shows a weak peak starting at ~T_0 and returning to baseline at
> ~T+20 sec. The main emission starts at ~T+25 sec with 2, or possibly 3, overlapping peaks, with
> the peak emission at ~T+33 sec, which then approximately exponentially decays out to 510 sec or
> possibly out to ~750 sec. At the 3- or 4-sigma level, there is a possible precursor peak at
> ~T-70 sec with a duration of 10-20 sec."*

GCN 12800 (Konus-Wind), verbatim:
> *"The burst light curve shows a single pulse with a duration of ~15 s. There is a weak precursor
> marginally seen at ~T0-30 s."*

GCN 12801 (GBM), verbatim:
> *"The GBM light curve consists of two main pulses with a duration (T90) of about 17 s (50-300 keV)."*

**Converted to OUR clock (GBM T0 = BAT T0 + 28.24 s = KW T0 + 1.71 s):**

| component | BAT clock | **GBM clock (ours)** | inside our stamped window [−1.5, +15.0] s? |
|---|---|---|---|
| possible 3–4σ precursor | ~T−70 s, 10–20 s long | **~−98 s** | no |
| weak first peak | T0 → T+20 s | **−28.2 → −8.2 s** | **no — and it lands inside our pre-burst background window [−30, −8] s** |
| main emission | T+25 s → decay to 510–750 s | **−3.2 s → +482 / +722 s** | partly: we cover −1.5 → +15.0 s |
| peak of main emission | ~T+33 s | **~+4.8 s** | yes (GBM: 64 ms peak flux from T0+4.8 s — independent confirmation of the clock conversion) |

This is the D4 pattern in its milder form: the trigger time is **not** the burst start, and the
GBM T0 sits on the *main* pulse. Two independent non-GCN corroborations of pre-trigger emission:
GBM catalog **T90_start = −11.52 s** and Golkhou+2015 **T_start,100 = −25.674 s**.

**Consensus note (Rossi+2026 §4.1 procedure, adopted in Phase 4).** Ep for the *same* physical
main pulse spans **276–805 keV** across GBM-Band, GBM-Comp, GBM-prelim-CPL and Konus-CPL. The
outlier is Konus (805 keV, 20 keV–4 MeV, CPL with β unconstrained: β < −2.04). The plausible
driver is model + band: a CPL with no high-energy power law pushes Ep up when the true β is
soft (β ≈ −2.0), and KW's 4 MeV ceiling with a poorly-constrained β makes it worse. **We do not
pick one.** Any correlation-plane placement of this burst must carry an inter-instrument spread
that dominates the statistical error. All per-instrument values are retained above for the P3 diff.

## 4. Instrument / mission story

- **Swift-BAT** triggered first (02:15:55 UT), partial coding 78%, ground position
  RA/Dec = 276.224, +24.713 ± 1.0′ (GCN 12799). Prompt slew.
- **Fermi-GBM** triggered 28.24 s later on the *main* pulse; on-ground location consistent with
  Swift; **LAT boresight angle 84°** (GCN 12801) — consistent with our poshist computation
  (θ_LATz = 87.2–87.9° across the source window). `HAS_LAT = False` in `results/grb_sample.ecsv`;
  no LAT/LLE data on disk. The 84–88° boresight explains it: the burst was outside the LAT FoV.
- **Konus-Wind** triggered independently; **other detections listed by von Kienlin+2014 Table 6:
  `K, R, IA, S, Me`** (Konus, RHESSI, INTEGRAL-SPI/ACS, Swift, MESSENGER) — an IPN-grade event.
- **Swift-XRT**: bright fading source; PC-mode Γ = 2.04 ± 0.08, N_H = 2.59 (+0.26, −0.25) e21 cm⁻²
  vs Galactic 1.0e21 (10.3σ excess); late light curve α = 1.99 (+0.27, −0.11) breaking at T+13.0 ks
  to α = 0.97 (+0.27, −0.22) (GCN 12797).
- **Swift-UVOT**: white(FC) = 18.10 ± 0.06 at 121–271 s; u = 18.52 ± 0.14; b, w1, m2, w2 upper
  limits (GCN 12796). E(B−V) = 0.13.
- GBM detector selection in the catalog: `bcat_detector_mask = 00011100000000`,
  `scat_detector_mask = 00011100000010` → **n3, n4, n5 + b0** — identical to our approved set.

## 7. Open issues / honest failures

1. **Lü+2022 PDF not obtained** (IOP bot captcha ×2; no arXiv). Sample membership confirmed by ADS
   full text only; no value extracted.
2. **Lu+2018 Table 2 row 4** ([6.13, 12.35] s): Ep = 232.48 ± 64.40 keV read, but α/β/flux/χ²ᵣ fall
   on the page break and were not extracted. Ep is the load-bearing number and is recorded.
3. **Burgess 2019 column order ambiguity (T4).** The PDF header prints
   `α_Band, α_SBPL, Δ_SBPL, δDIC, p_eff^Band, p_eff^SBPL, p_B^Band, p_B^synch`, but the body order
   is almost certainly `…, Δ_SBPL, p_eff^Band, p_eff^SBPL, δDIC, p_B^Band, p_B^synch` (position 6 is
   negative for several bursts, position 4–5 are always ≈3–35). α_Band, α_SBPL, Δ_SBPL and the two
   p_B values are unambiguous; **δDIC = −4.15 vs p_eff = 9.97/3.38 carries this caveat.**
4. **Lag sign convention clash** between Lu+2018 (τ̂ > 0, high energy leads) and our catalog
   (LAG = −0.0515 s at 117σ). Recorded, unresolved (T5/L26).
5. **Background-window contamination risk (new; not in `results/human_review_qc_flags.txt`).**
   Our pre-burst background is [−30, −8] s; three independent sources place emission there —
   BAT's weak first peak (GBM −28.2 → −8.2 s), GBM catalog T90_start = −11.52 s,
   Golkhou+2015 T_start,100 = −25.674 s. The GBM catalog's own pre-burst background stops at
   **−28.5 s** (`back_interval_low` = [−32.0, −28.5]), i.e. it does *not* extend into that region.
   Burgess 2019 §2 records hitting the same class of problem generically:
   *"It was required that some background selections be modified as the ones specified in the
   online catalog occasionally contained on-source intervals."*
   This is a **selection question for Vikas**, not a unilateral change: the selection is
   human-approved and Stage-1 flags are decisions. Raised, not acted on.
6. Two `Skills_training/*.pdf` files could not be opened during the corpus dedup grep
   (`Hurley_2017_2017ApJS22931H_bn101126198.pdf`, `You_2026_2026AA710A125Y_bn101126198.pdf`) —
