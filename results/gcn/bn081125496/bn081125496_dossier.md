## Extracted claims (all from GCN 8553, verbatim quotes on file)
- **Position:** RA=42.3, Dec=−22.4 (J2000), ±1.0° (1σ stat) + 2–3° syst [GBM].
- **Light curve:** "single peak" — consistent with our single-pulse selection.
- **T90 (8–1000 keV) ≈ 15 s; T50 ≈ 4.4 s** (preliminary).
- **Spectrum, T0→T0+3.200 s:** Band, **Epeak = 221±16 keV, α = +0.14±0.09,
  β = −2.34±0.22** (χ²=354/357).
- **Spectrum, T0+3.200→T0+14.464 s:** "power law function with an exponential
  high energy cutoff" (= CPL/Comptonized), **index −0.27±0.06, Epeak =
  257±12 keV** (χ²=360/358).
- **Fluence (8–1000 keV, T0+0.003→T0+14.464 s): (4.91±0.16)e−5 erg/cm²**;
  1-s peak flux 27.0±2.7 ph/s/cm² from T0+2.176 s.

## Flags for downstream steps
1. **α = +0.14 ± 0.09 in the first 3.2 s** — a POSITIVE low-energy index, far
   above the synchrotron slow-cooling limit (−2/3): a hard-spectrum burst.
   Watch the early bins in Step 6 (photosphere-candidate territory; the
   extra_lowE_curvature class will be interesting here).
2. **Fluence discrepancy:** GCN preliminary 4.91e−5 vs our catalog (grb_sample/
   BCAT) 1.9e−5 (8–1000 keV vs catalog convention TBD) — factor ~2.6.
   Preliminary-vs-final is normal; do NOT mix the two anywhere.
3. **T90 discrepancy:** GCN ~15 s vs catalog 9.3 s — same class of difference.
4. Spectral evolution already visible in the circular itself: Band(α=+0.14)
   → CPL(−0.27), Epeak roughly constant 221→257 keV — a first hint for the
   evolution step.
