## ⭐ REDSHIFT = 2.1974 ± 0.0002 — **SECURE, and our table was UPGRADED**
- **de Ugarte Postigo et al. 2013, A&A 557, L18** (`2013A&A...557L..18D`, arXiv:1309.1167),
  *"The obscured hyper-energetic GRB 120624B hosted by a luminous compact galaxy at z=2.20"*:
  *"we detect emission lines of [O II], [O III] and H-α at a common redshift of
  z = 2.1974 ± 0.0002, which we identify as the redshift of the host galaxy and, consequently
  of the GRB."* Instrument: **X-shooter/VLT**, 17.1–19.1 d post-burst.
- ⚠ **NO redshift appears in ANY circular** — the circulars offer only *z > ~0.3* inferred from
  the Amati relation (GCN 13389) and photometric **galaxy** z of 0.43–0.57 (GCN 13395), both of
  which would have been misleading. The real z came a year later, in the dedicated paper.
- **Our `results/redshifts.ecsv` had z=2.1974 tagged `GRBweb-compilation / confidence=compilation`
  — the value was right but UNSOURCED. Now upgraded to the primary reference + method + error.**

**Our stamped source window is [−1.16, +27.11] s ≈ 28 s — about 10% of T90.** Far more extreme
than burst #2's truncation (12.8 vs 26.8 s). So our "single pulse" is the MAIN PULSE of a much
longer burst. This is a defensible single-pulse selection (Busby selects shape) but it must be
stated explicitly: our T90/fluence/E_iso are **main-pulse quantities**, not burst-integrated, and
they are NOT comparable to the published 271 s / E_iso=3.0e54 without that caveat.
