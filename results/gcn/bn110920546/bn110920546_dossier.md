### 4.6 The dedicated paper — Iyyani+2015, `2015MNRAS.450.1651I`

## 5. What the literature *wants* our prompt spectrum to be (disclosure for P3)

Three mutually exclusive published readings of the **same** data exist, and each was defended
in a refereed journal:
1. **Band + BB, two emission zones** — McGlynn 2012 (kT = 61.3 keV integrated over 0–52.7 s;
   kT decays ∼100 → ∼40 keV; BB is 20–40 % of the 10–10 000 keV flux in 19 of 25 bins).
2. **Slow-cooled synchrotron + BB** — Burgess+2014a/b (F_BB/F_tot = 0.39; magnetic jet).
3. **Pure photosphere: Comptonised 2BB (+PL)** — Iyyani+2015, best model, τ ∼ 20 subphotospheric
   dissipation; explicitly says the Band function CANNOT fit the data
   (*"the wavy structure of the residuals"*).
And a fourth, negative: **Yu+2016** finds the thermal component NOT significant against a
**PL** continuum by their simulated ΔCSTAT_crit.
Iyyani 2018 Fig. 7b shows (2) and (3) fitting the same data with **different spectral shapes**.
⇒ Our blind fit will inevitably land near one of these; the bias to disclose is that this
burst is a **famous photosphere showcase**, so a thermal-friendly result is the *expected*
answer and must be defended on our own statistics, not on agreement.
