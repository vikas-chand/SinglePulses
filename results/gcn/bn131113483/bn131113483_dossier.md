## 6. ⚠ THE THING THAT ACTUALLY MATTERS: this is an EARTH-LIMB burst

Computed locally in this pass from `data/bn131113483/glg_poshist_all_131113_v01.fit` (POS_X/Y/Z,
geocentre direction = −POS) and the tcat position; not quoted from anywhere.

|---|---|---|---|
| −115 (bkg_neg start) | 61.56° | 66.97° | **−5.41° (OCCULTED)** |
| −40 | 65.98° | 66.98° | **−1.00° (OCCULTED)** |
| **−23.14** | — | — | **LIMB CROSSING — source rises** |
| −9 (bkg_neg stop) | 67.82° | 66.98° | +0.83° |
| −1 (SRC_START) | 68.29° | 66.98° | +1.31° |
| +3.39 (T90 start) | 68.53° | 66.98° | +1.55° |
| +33 (peak) | 70.31° | 66.99° | +3.32° |
| +62 (SRC_STOP) | 72.03° | 66.99° | +5.04° |
| +172 (bkg_pos stop) | 78.58° | 67.00° | +11.58° |

Rise rate ≈ **0.059° s⁻¹**. Spacecraft altitude 550.6–551.6 km; `FLAGS = 0` (no SAA) throughout.

**Two consequences, both falsifiable:**

1. **The pre-burst background window is split by an occultation edge.** The stamped
   `BKG_NEG = [−115, −9] s` spends its first **92 of 106 s** (87%) with the source direction
   *behind the Earth*, and the limb crossing at −23.1 s sits inside it. The Earth's disc is
   sweeping through the detectors' field of view across the whole window, so the background is
   changing geometrically, not just secularly. **PREDICT:** the pre-burst polynomial residuals
   show structure/curvature around t ≈ −23 s. This is the bn110920546-SAA lesson in a different
   key: the background model, not the response, is the risk.

2. **The burst itself may be seen THROUGH the upper atmosphere for its first ~15 s.** The
   *geometric* limb (0 km) is crossed at −23.1 s, but X-rays are absorbed by the atmosphere
   well above the solid Earth. Crossing times for an absorbing shell of height h:
   | h | limb radius | source rises at |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0 km (geometric) | 66.96° | **−23.1 s** |
   | 50 km | 68.04° | **−4.8 s** |
   | 100 km | 69.17° | **+14.4 s** |
   | 120 km | 69.64° | **+22.3 s** |

   The transition is **energy-dependent** (higher energies penetrate to lower tangent heights),
   so if it bites, it bites as a *time-varying, energy-dependent low-energy deficit* over the
   first ~10–20 s — exactly the interval whose archival Band α is the hardest of the burst
   (α = +0.06 in block 0, softening to −0.74 by block 4). **This is a live alternative
   explanation for hard early α on this burst, and no published work has ever considered it.**
   ⚠ It is a *hypothesis with a quantified geometry*, not an established fact: the effective
   absorbing height for 8–100 keV photons at these tangent geometries has NOT been computed here,
   and the effect may well be negligible by +3 s. It must be tested, not assumed — see the P0
   predictions P-D1/P-D2 in `notes/reconciliation/bn131113483_P0_frozen.json`.

### 7.4 Frame-alignment labels attached to every number above (L21 / Phase 4)
- **Ep convention:** RMFIT `band` and `comp` both report **Ep = νFν peak in keV**. Our pipeline's
  `CPL_XC` is the **e-folding energy**; convert with `Ep = (2 + index)·xc` before any comparison.
  (Sanity check: archival integrated CPL xc = 104.87, index = −0.385 → Ep = 169.4 keV, against the
  catalogue's 160.6 keV over a longer window — consistent for a hard-to-soft burst.)
- **SBPL `PARAM3` is the break energy, not Ep**, with `brkscale = 0.3` **fixed**.
- **Index sign:** photon-index convention, α/β negative for falling spectra (same as ours).
- **Energy bands:** NaI fit channels 4/5–124 (≈4.1–2000 keV), BGO channels 3–89 (≈115–50000 keV)
  per the scat `DETECTOR DATA` extension; note the catalogue did **not** exclude the 30–40 keV
  K-edge, whereas Bordoloi+2026 and our pipeline do.
- **T0:** all times relative to `TRIGTIME = 406035340.15746` (2013-11-13T11:35:37.157 UTC),
  **except Konus's**, which are relative to 41768.195 s UT = T0 + 31.04 s.
- **Detector set:** b0,n1,n2,n5 for every catalogue fit — identical to ours.
