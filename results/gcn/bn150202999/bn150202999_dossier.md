## 3. Instrument story / what the data are

- **Two emission episodes, only one of them bright.** Episode 1 = a single hard pulse from ≈ T0
  to ≈ T0+25 s peaking at ≈ 8 s (GBM `flux_1024_time = 7.808 s`, `flux_64_time = 8.128 s`).
  Episode 2 = a faint, soft, ~100-s-long tail of emission from ≈ T0+100 s to ≈ T0+210 s carrying
  only **4.2 %** of episode 1's fluence (1.37e−6 vs 3.15e−5 erg cm⁻², GCN 17388). Verified by eye
  on `plots/lightcurves/bn150202999_lc.png` (n0): a single sharp spike 0–20 s reaching ~3× the
  background, then a barely-visible bump around 100–160 s.
- **Detectors.** The GBM catalog used **NaI 0, 1, 3 + BGO 0** (`bcat_detector_mask 11010000000000`
  = NaI 0/1/3; `scat_detector_mask 11010000000010` = + BGO 0; von Kienlin+2020 p.131 duration row
  lists the detector set as `0+1+3`). **Our approved set is identical plus LLE:**
  n0 (9.17°), n1 (30.78°), n3 (40.31°), b0 (74.08°), lle — `results/background_intervals.ecsv`,
  APPROVED_BY *Vikas Chand*, 2026-07-19T22:18:36Z, `human_gui`. Duan & Wang 2020 used a *smaller*
  set (n0, n1, b0 — no n3).
- **LLE is real and it is inside our window.** 2FLGC: `lle_bbbd_sig = 20.0` (σ), `lle_t05 = 4.862`,
  `lle_t90 = 12.648`, `lle_t95 = 17.510` s. Our stamped window (−3 → +22 s) **contains the entire
  LLE emission**.
- **The >100 MeV LAT emission is OUTSIDE our window (D4).** 2FLGC: `tl0 = 44.86 s`,
  `tl1 = 115.70 s`, `tl100 = 70.84 ± 54.49 s`; highest-energy associated event **1 GeV at
  t = 115.70 s** with association probability 0.99. Our window closes at +22 s, i.e. **23 s before
  the LAT emission begins**. Per DataInventory D4 a detection outside the window predicts nothing
  inside it — in either direction.
- **No slew.** The ARR was *rejected* by the spacecraft (GCN 17388), so the boresight angle is
  essentially constant (θ = 16.9–17.1° over our window, computed from `gll_pt`), and the
  single-matrix LLE DRM is valid (Δθ = 0.13°, §6).
- **No SAA contamination.** `IN_SAA` is False throughout −95 → +131 s (`glg_poshist_all_150202_v01.fit`).
- **Multi-instrument:** Fermi (GBM+LAT/LLE), Konus-Wind, MESSENGER GRNS, INTEGRAL SPI-ACS,
  Swift-BAT (out of coded FoV), Swift-XRT/UVOT (afterglow). No optical afterglow, no host, no z.

## 7. Open inconsistencies — recorded, NOT resolved (T5 / L26)

1. **Is there a GeV photon or isn't there?** Nava 2018 Table 1 lists the highest-energy photon as
   **"< 0.1"** GeV with no arrival time, consistent with GCN 17385's own reading (*"this detection
   is likely due to low energy gamma-rays (below 100 MeV)"*). 2FLGC lists
   `ext_emission_max_ene = 1000 MeV` at t = 115.70 s with probability 0.99, and a standard-analysis
   TS = 29. Both are "published"; they disagree. **Impact on us: none inside our window** (both
   epochs lie ≥ 23 s after we stop), but it must not be laundered into a claim either way.
2. **β at the ~2σ level across eras/intervals:** GBM catalog TOTAL β = −2.149 ± 0.106, GBM team
   EP1 β = −2.36 ± 0.13, KW EP1 β = −2.39 (+0.15/−0.22, 90 % CL), Duan & Wang 0–50 s
   β = −2.260 ± 0.166. The *wider*-interval catalog value is the *shallowest*, which is the opposite
   of what adding the soft EP2 should do. Left open.
3. **Lag sign for this burst:** Duan & Wang 2020 place 150202B among the bursts expected to show
   **negative** lags (from Ep/flux peak alignment); Maraventano+2025 **measure** τ₁₂ and τ₁₃
   **positive** at ≥3σ and only τ₁₄ (LLE band) negative. Prediction vs measurement, different
   quantities — but they are cited in the same breath in the earlier
   `notes/literature_tier1/bn150202999.md`, so the distinction is flagged here.
4. **Nava's fluence band is unverified** — the Table-1 footnote could not be recovered from the
   PDF text layer.
