**Frame notes (Phase 4, L21):**
1. Catalog/GCN Ep are the νFν-peak (SCAT PARAM1) convention; SBPL quotes a BREAK energy
   (29.5 keV flnc) — do not diff Ebreak against Ep (Ep(Band) 41.7 vs Ebreak(SBPL) 29.5 is a
   convention difference, not a discrepancy).
2. **Very soft burst.** Every integrated frame agrees: Ep(Band) ≈ 42–46 keV, α ≈ −0.9…−1.0,
   β ≈ −2.31, consistent GCN↔catalog↔our archival run (42.7/−0.93/−2.30). The Band-vs-Comp χ²
   gap (1.328 vs 1.432 over ~357 dof) says the high-energy power law is REQUIRED — β is
   well-measured, not railed.
3. Detector-set frames: catalog spectral fits used **b1, n6, na**; Yu+2019 used **n6, na, b1**;
   we add **n9** (6.7°, best-viewing). Carry as a frame difference.
4. Multi-frame T90 spread (6.6 / 22.5 / 7.94 s) is COMPONENT COVERAGE + algorithm, not physics:
   the bcat 22.5 s spans weak-pulse→main-pulse; Qin+2013's 7.94 s and the GCN's 6.6 s
   effectively describe the main pulse. Qin+2013's own per-band values show the usual
   T90 narrowing with energy (9.15 → 5.34 s from 15 keV to 350 keV).
5. Evolution-class consensus across eras: **intensity-tracking** (Lu+2012 "late-pulse tracking";
   Lu+2018 tracking, positive kE; Yu+2019 "i.t. to ?"; Li 2026 Type I aligned-peaks with
   tp(Ep) = tp(F) = 21.26 s and ρ(Ep,F) = 0.90). No frame claims monotonic hard-to-soft.
