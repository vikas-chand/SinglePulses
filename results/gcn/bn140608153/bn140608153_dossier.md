### 6.5 Convention footnotes (L21 / Phase 4)
- GBM `comp`/`band` Eₚ is the **νFν peak in keV**, not an e-folding energy; our pipeline's
  `CPL_XC` is the **e-folding energy Ec**, so the comparable quantity is
  **Eₚ = (2 + index)·Ec**.
- GBM indices are photon-index sign convention (α negative = soft), same as ours.
- All GBM catalog times are relative to the same T0 = `TRIGTIME` = MET 423891663.976584
  that our pipeline uses; **no T0 offset correction is needed** for this burst.
- Multi-instrument consensus (Rossi+2026 rule) is **not applicable**: only one instrument
  ever measured this burst, so there is no inter-instrument spread to fold into the error.
  Its Eₚ error bar is a *single-fit* error and should be treated as a floor.

## 7. WHAT THE SLEW DOES TO THE ANALYSIS (feeds Steps 5/6 and the QC file)

1. **Response validity: PASS.** Every approved detector resolves through the production
   glob `glg_cspec_<det>_*.rsp*` to a **20-matrix `.rsp2`** spanning **−40.96 → +98.31 s**
   relative to TRIGTIME, which brackets the stamped source window (−4.0 → +78.0 s) on both
   sides. The multi-matrix response exists precisely to track this slew (~2.3° of motion
   per matrix at 0.331 °/s) — this burst is the *reason* `.rsp2` files are the default.
2. **Detector quality is time-dependent.** `na` starts at 39.97° and ends at **64.10°**,
   crossing the project's ≤50° NaI preference at t ≈ +35 s; `n0` improves from 48.59° to
   22.67°. The approved five-detector set is defensible at every epoch — but no single
   epoch's angles describe it. Nothing is dropped (data-dropping hierarchy, case 1).
3. **No detector is missing.** The only NaI that could compete is n2 (50.6° at T0), and it
   *worsens* monotonically through the slew (53.7° by +20 s), so the six downloaded
   detectors cover every epoch's best geometry. No download gap.
4. **BGO choice differs from the catalog.** We approved **b0** (98.0° at T0 → 85.5° at
   +78 s); the GBM catalog used **b1** (82.0° → 94.5°), the BGO on the same side as the
   dominant NaIs n9/na. `b1`'s TTE/CSPEC/rsp2 are on disk and unused. Recorded as a
   standing human Stage-1 decision, not re-flagged — but it is the first thing to check if
   the BGO cross-normalisation misbehaves (the archival prior already shows `cons_b0`
   railing at its 0.800 floor in block 2).
5. **Background is clean.** The nearest SAA passage ended **3288 s before** the trigger and
   the next began **1468 s after** (poshist `FLAGS` bit 1, whose 13.4 % duty cycle and
   lat −25.5…+1.9° / lon 284–360°+0–8° footprint identify it as the SAA bit). This is the
   *opposite* of the bn110920546 situation — no activation-decay tail here.
