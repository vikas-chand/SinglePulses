This burst ships **only single-matrix `.rsp` files** (v01; zero `.rsp2`). Per **D1**, a
single-matrix DRM's validity domain is set by the **off-axis angle**, not by TSTART/TSTOP —
and this is exactly the slewing-spacecraft case D1 was written for.

- **Source window −1 → +15 s: responses VALID.** Max Δθ = **0.19°** (n7, n9) against a 4°
  threshold. The slew begins ~37 s *after* the window closes. **PASS.**
- **⚠ Post-background window +25 → +135 s crosses the slew.** Boresight moves 10.67°;
  n6 swings 20.43° → 12.25°, b1 55.93° → 63.46°. Roughly +25…+52 s is attitude-stable,
  +52…+135 s is not. A polynomial background assumes a rate-stationary detector orientation;
  Earth-limb/albedo geometry tracks attitude, so that assumption is false over ~80 of the
  110 s of post-burst background. **Flag only** — the selection is human-approved and stays;
  the recommended diagnostic is a Step-5/6 refit with the post window truncated to +25…+50 s.
