**Frame notes for the P3 diff:**
1. Spectral-catalog detector set = **b1,n6,n9**; OUR approved set = **n6,n7,n9,nb,b1**
   (adds n7,nb; na on disk but NOT approved). Duration-catalog set = n6,n7,n9,na,nb.
   Three different sets — never diff across them silently.
2. Catalog flnc interval [−90.1, +17.4] s vs our stamped [−13, +16] s: their Ep≈41 keV is
   diluted by ~77 s of weak pre-trigger emission; our window isolates the trigger pulse
   (pflx Ep≈73–78 keV is the closer frame for our brightest bins).
3. This is a **soft, weak-peak burst** (Ep,flnc ≈ 41 keV — near the bottom of the GBM band;
   1024-ms peak flux 5.46 ph/cm²/s). Ep caps/floors are not a concern, but low-Ep
   parametrization (Ep close to fit-range edge 8 keV × curvature) may stress CPL/Band fits.
4. β≈−2.2 well-constrained in flnc Band — a genuine high-energy power law is present in the
   catalog frame (BGO b1 signal).
