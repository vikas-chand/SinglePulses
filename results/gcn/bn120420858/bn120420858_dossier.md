## 3. Instrument story

* **GBM triggered** on 2012-04-20 20:35:13.07, algorithm 11 (512 ms, 50–300 keV
  trigger band), catalog localization band 47–291 keV (von Kienlin+2020 table4).
* **Duration detectors** n4 + n8 (von Kienlin+2020 table5 `Det = 4+8`), the same
  two NaIs our Stage-1 selection approved.
* **LAT: outside the field of view at trigger.** Boresight angle
  θ = 109.71°, zenith ζ = 76.77°, `arr_flag = 0` (no autonomous repoint)
  — HEASARC `fermilgrb`. Our own poshist recomputation gives θ = 110.4° → 111.9°
  across −2…+48 s at the LAT position (107.3° → 108.9° at the GBM position),
  confirming the catalog value.
* **LAT detection exists but is ~1 hour late.** 2FLGC `TLAT,0 = 3501.67 s`,
  `TLAT,1 = 3908.01 s`, `TLAT,100 = 406.34 ± 13.96 s`. The LAT likelihood in the
  **GBM window is not merely non-detected but not even fitted**:
  `like_gbm_ts = 0.0` with all `like_gbm_*` fields zero. Highest-energy associated
  photon: **1.9 GeV at t = 3800.54 s** (P = 1.0), one of only 4 events.
  This is the **D4 case in its purest form** — `HAS_LAT = True` in our sample
  table, yet nothing the LAT saw lies inside our −2…+48 s window.
* **LLE: none.** `lle_bbbd_sig = 0.0`, `lle_bbbd_sig_detected = 0` (fermilgrb);
  2FLGC Table 3 gives `−  −  −` for TLLE,05/95/90. No `gll_*` file exists on
  disk. Consistent with θ ≈ 110°: LLE needs the source in the LAT FoV.
* **Afterglow: none reported anywhere** (no circulars, no follow-up paper).

## 5. BEYOND-PROMPT CONTEXT (future modules)

Thin, but real. The **LAT late emission at 3502–3908 s** (TS ≈ 28, index ≈ −2.07,
1.9 GeV photon at 3800 s) is an **afterglow-module** target, not a prompt one: it
begins ~13 T90 after trigger and there is no contemporaneous prompt counterpart.
Tag `module-future; afterglow`. Two caveats a future afterglow module must
inherit: (i) the LAT only sees the position after Fermi's rocking brings it into
the FoV, so the "onset" at 3501.7 s is an **exposure** boundary, not a physical
one; (ii) with TS ≈ 28 and 4 photons, and a 4.4° GBM/LAT position offset, the
association itself deserves re-checking before anything is built on it.
No SN / cosmology / multimessenger material exists (no z, no IPN row found).

## 6. Step-1 data inventory & QC — summary (full record: `results/qc/bn120420858_step1_response_coverage.ecsv`)

**Stamped window** (`results/background_intervals.ecsv`, APPROVED_BY Vikas Chand,
2026-07-19T22:18:36Z, `human_gui`, WINDOW_SOURCE `adjusted` for n4/n8/b0 and
`accepted_suggestion` for b1): **SRC −2.0 … +48.0 s**, detectors n4, n8, b0, b1.

**Manifest:** cspec ×4, tte ×4, rsp2 ×4, poshist ×1, trigdat ×1, LLE 0, LAT 2
(+ `LAT/blocks/block_0`, `block_1` staging dirs). **Responses: all four detectors
resolve to a 13-matrix `.rsp2` spanning −7.936 … +295.173 s** — brackets the
stamped source window with room to spare. **Verdict: PASS_WITH_WARN.**

* **WARN-1 (severity HIGH — component coverage / D4·T9).** Our −2…+48 s window
  captures only ~19% of T90 and **~40–43% of the 50–300 keV net counts**. Emission
  is still significant far past the window: with a background polynomial fitted
  ONLY outside 0–320 s (deduplicated CSPEC bins, deg 2 and deg 3 agree to <3%),
  n4/n8 net significance runs 48–60 s: 8.2/10.8σ · 60–80 s: 11.7/14.3σ ·
  80–120 s: 14.1/16.2σ · 120–160 s: 8.0/13.1σ · 160–200 s: 7.0/8.1σ ·
  200–255 s: 6.2/9.1σ. The catalog T90 = 254.9 s and T50START = 21.5 s are real,
  not a background artifact. **Consequence: every "time-integrated" number we
  produce is the integral of the first 50 s of a 255 s burst, and must be labelled
  as such in any comparison with the catalog fluence spectrum.**
* **WARN-2 (severity HIGH — background contamination).** All four stamped POST
  background windows sit **on** that tail: b1 58.0–189.5 s, b0 87.6–277.6 s,
  n4 114.6–249.6 s, n8 131.6–243.6 s. Fitting the background there biases it
  high and suppresses net source counts inside the window. The PRE windows
  (−396…−10 s) are clean. `notes/codex_bkg_review.md` already graded this burst
  QUESTIONABLE but under-stated the tail as "52–55 s" — because that review used
  the same contaminated background. **The human approval stands (Stage-1 flags
  ARE decisions; there is no accepted flag row for this burst in
  `results/human_review_qc_flags.txt`) — this is recorded, not overruled.**
* **WARN-3 (position).** Detector angles are computed at the GBM position. At the
  2FLGC LAT position (4.44° away) n4 would run 47.1° → 50.9°, crossing the 50°
  NaI preference. Recorded; no action.
* **INFO (LAT).** The downloaded LAT file covers −250 … +1500 s (P8R3 transient,
  12° ROI, 30 MeV–300 GeV) and contains **zero events**. Combined with θ ≈ 108°
  and `like_gbm_ts = 0`, the LAT plugin must not be used for this burst; GBM-only
  is the correct configuration, and the 2FLGC detection at 3502–3908 s predicts
  nothing about our window in either direction (D4).
* **INFO (no D1 check).** No LLE files → the single-matrix off-axis test does not
  apply. All four responses are multi-matrix `.rsp2`, so interval coverage is the
  right test and it passes.
* **INFO (a trap I fell into, recorded so the next worker does not).** CSPEC for
  this burst ships **overlapping** records at the 1.024 s → 4.096 s resolution
  boundary (e.g. a 4.096 s bin 124.162–128.258 s duplicating the fine bins
  underneath it). A naive rate curve double-counts exposure there and produces a
  spurious ~15% coherent "drop" in **all four detectors** near 120–140 s. It is
  an artifact of the reader, not of the data; deduplicate by start time before
  computing any rate.
