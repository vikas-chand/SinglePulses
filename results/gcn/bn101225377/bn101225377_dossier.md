## 🔴 T1 — the single most dangerous fact about this burst
`GRB 101225A` is a **different event**: the famous **"Christmas burst"**, Swift-BAT
trigger 441015, **2010-12-25 18:37:45 UT** (9 h 35 m *after* ours), RA = 0.234,
Dec = +44.602 (GCN 11493) — **47.52°** from our position. It owns
- all **27** circulars on `https://gcn.gsfc.nasa.gov/other/101225.gcn3`, and
- all **165** ADS full-text hits for `full:"GRB 101225A"` (Ror+2024, Kann+2024,
  Thöne+2011, … an entire ultra-long-GRB / magnetar / minor-body literature).

**Not one of them is about our burst.** Any number harvested from "GRB 101225A"
would be contamination. Our burst is identified **by trigger only**
(`bn101225377` / MET 314960575 / 2010-12-25T09:02:53.498 UTC).

## Instrument story
GBM triggered at 09:02:53.498 UT with on-board detectors **n8 + nb**
(`DET_MASK = 00000000100100`) — **yet no excess is visible at the trigger in any
detector.** The GRB itself arrives **≈76 s later**:

- n8 TTE (8–900 keV, 2-s bins, background from the stamped windows): flat at
  ≈1190 c/s from −28 s to +74 s, rises at **+76 s**, peaks ≈1650–1790 c/s over
  **+80…+95 s**, decays to background by **≈+108 s**. One broad, single pulse.
- Fine search around t = 0 (0.064-s bins, and 0.5-s bins in 8–30 / 30–100 /
  100–900 keV, for n7, n8 and nb): **nothing above ≈2σ**. TTE starts at −27.8 s,
  so a pre-trigger episode before that cannot be excluded from TTE alone.
- `trigdat` 8.19-s bins, **all 14 detectors**: the excess appears **only** in the
  77.83 / 86.02 / 94.21 / 102.40 s bins.
- The on-board position is 50° from the ground position — consistent with the
  on-board solution not having been driven by the real source.

Detector geometry at the pulse (computed here with the production estimator, the
gtburst `angularDistance` port in `scripts/00_prototype_one_burst.py`):
**nb 26.6° · n8 28.9° · b1 30.3° · n7 54.7°**, then n6 79.3°, n9 85.0°, na 85.3°.
The four approved detectors are the four closest — and are **exactly** the catalog
`scat` set (mask `00000001100101` → n7, n8, nb, b1; `bcat` mask → n7, n8, nb).
Per-detector S/N over the stamped window from TTE: **nb 63.8σ, n8 59.1σ,
n7 40.6σ** (8–900 keV) and **b1 10.8σ** (200–40 000 keV) — signal present in every
approved detector, ordered as the geometry predicts. No Earth occultation at any
time from −95 s to +205 s (geocentre angle 126–131°, Earth radius 66.7°).

**⚠ D4-inverted (record it, it matters):** the trigger time is **76 s BEFORE** the
burst, not the burst start. Any statement anchored on T0 for this burst is anchored
75+ s early.
