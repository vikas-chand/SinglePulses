## 5. BEYOND-PROMPT CONTEXT (future modules) — Phase 3c

* **Swift-XRT afterglow (GCN 18237, 18243).** Source 1 at 214.9796, −69.3536 (4.3″), first
  detected T0+59.6 ks, PC-mode decay index α_X = 1.0 (+0.7, −0.4); Source 2 (215.2707, −69.4918)
  never identified. No UVOT counterpart (white > 21.7, v > 20.3, u > 20.7; E(B−V) = 0.40).
  → `module-future; afterglow`.
* **He, Tam & Long 2022 (A&A 657, A111)** — searched all Fermi-LAT afterglows (t > 2×T90,
  0.1–200 GeV) for a high-energy upturn. GRB 150902A is one of six bursts where a broken
  power law beats a single PL only **tentatively/marginally** (3 < ΔTS_BPL−PL < 9, upturn above
  E_break ≈ 1 GeV); the significant case is GRB 131231A alone (ΔTS = 13.63, ~3.7σ).
  Their §2.4 re-runs T0→T0+2×T90 to look for the same upturn **in the prompt phase**: only
  090902B, 130427A and 190114C had enough photons — **150902A did not**, so nothing in that
  paper constrains our prompt high-energy shape. They also assume z = 1 for a CTA/MAGIC
  detectability figure (an assumption, §1). → `module-future; afterglow` + one prompt-relevant null.
* **Fraija et al. 2026 (MNRAS 545, 1970)** — reverse-shock SSC closure relations with varying
  microphysical parameters, applied to 2FLGC bursts; GRB 150902A appears in Fig. A7 as one of
  the (α, β) panels tested against the ISM/wind × thick/thin-shell closure relations. No prompt
  numbers. → `module-future; afterglow`.
* **Ershova et al. 2020 (Astron. Rep. 64, 126)** — MASTER early optical observations of GRBs
  compared with their γ/X properties; GRB 150902A enters through the MASTER-SAAO upper limits
  (GCN 18234). Not open access; recorded from the ADS full-text hit + the circular.
  → `module-future; afterglow; optical`.
* **Chen et al. 2024 (ApJ 972, 132 — GRB 231129C) and Chen et al. 2024 (ApJ 964, 45 — GRB 220304A)**
  cite Nawaz & Sajjad 2022 as precedent for thermally-assisted / photospheric prompt spectra.
  They are comparison context for the BB claim, not analyses of this burst.
* **Nava 2018 (IJMPD 27, 1842003)** — review of GRB high-energy emission; mentions 150902A in
  the LAT-detected population. Context only.

## 6. What the literature *wants* our prompt spectrum to be (disclosed bias, Phase 3c)

The only dedicated paper (Nawaz & Sajjad 2022) is a **photospheric/magnetisation** paper: its
whole physical argument (ε_Th ≤ 0.5, magnetic reconnection vs internal shocks, initial radius
constraints) rests on a sub-dominant blackbody being real in both the time-integrated and the
time-resolved spectra. Two later papers (Chen+2024a,b) cite it as an exemplar of the same class.
Meanwhile Li & Zhang 2021 and Tak+2023 come at the same burst from the **high-latitude-emission**
side, with no BB at all in their models. Our blind fit must be reconciled against *both* frames,
and the P3 diff must state which continuum each published kT/Ep belongs to (T7).
