## Instrument story (per circular, verbatim-anchored)
- **GCN 23038 — Insight-HXMT/HE detection** (Nang et al., 2018-07-26): found in routine
  galactic-plane survey search. *"light curve mainly consists of a bright pulse followed by
  a tail"*. CsI low-HV ("GRB mode"), record energy ~200–3000 keV: **T90 = 23.2 s from
  T0+1.6 s**; 1-s peak rate 1312.4 cts/s at T0+3.7 s; 15779.6 total counts. HXMT NaI
  (~100–800 keV): **T90 = 31.1 s from T0+1.6 s**; 1-s peak rate 239.3 cts/s at T0+7.8 s;
  2859.7 total counts. Explicitly *preliminary*.
- **GCN 23100 — AstroSat CZTI detection** (Sharma et al., 2018-08-08): clear detection,
  40–200 keV: *"a single peak of emission with peak at 19:22:37.5 UT"* (= T0+6.5 s); peak
  585 cts/s above background (4 quadrants combined), 8613 total cts, mean bkg 673.9 cts/s;
  **T90 = 30.3 s (cumulative rates)**; 786 Compton events (polarization-capable count);
  also detected in CsI Veto, 100–500 keV.
