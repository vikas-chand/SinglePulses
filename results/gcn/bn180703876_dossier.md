## Redshift (verbatim, GCN 23889 — Izzo et al., VLT/MUSE)
> "we identify three strong emission lines which can be consistently interpreted as [O III] 4960,5008 AA
> and [O II] 3727/29 AA at a common redshift of z = 0.6678. Given the spatial coincidence with the optical
> counterpart, we propose that these features come from the GRB host galaxy and we conclude that this is
> the redshift of GRB 180703A."
Method: host-galaxy emission lines (not afterglow absorption); observed 10.92 h post-burst. ⚠ Chang+2026's
table prints z=0.6687 — treat as their typo; GCN 23889 is the source.

## Instrument story
Fermi-GBM trigger → Fermi-LAT detection (GCN 22883: "significant increase in the event rate…";
**highest-energy photon ~930 MeV at T0+40 s** — 7 s AFTER our stamped window closes) → Swift tiled XRT
(GCN 22882/22891) → afterglow confirmed fading, α>0.8 (GCN 22931) → LCO optical candidate i′=20.6±0.3 at
5.65 h (GCN 22892), confirmed as afterglow by fading (GCN 22944) → UVOT upper limits ~17.3 ks (GCN 22898)
→ MASTER limits (GCN 22926) → IPN triangulation, Konus-HEND annulus consistent with LAT position
(GCN 22925; Swift-BAT outside coded FoV) → Konus-Wind spectroscopy (GCN 22927) → VLT/MUSE z (GCN 23889).
