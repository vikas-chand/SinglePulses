## Independent detection (refereed, instrument-demo)
**BepiColombo MGNS** detected GRB 191125A in cruise; it is THE example light curve in
Mangano et al. 2021, SSRv 217, 23 (`2021SSRv..217...23M`, §MGNS cruise science): *"Fig. 9 Time
profile of gamma-ray burst GRB191125A detected by MGNS gamma-ray spectrometer (1 s acquisition
integrated counts)"* — in the context of MGNS *"participation in the InterPlanetary Network
(IPN) program"*. No spectral/temporal numbers are given. PDF (OSTI OA mirror):
`Skills_training/Mangano_2021_2021SSRv21723M_bn191125206.pdf`.
