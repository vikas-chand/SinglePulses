## ⚠ NAMING CAVEAT — the T1 same-day trap, letter-name edition
2010-06-12 had **three** GBM triggers of interest:
| trigger | UT | what |
|---|---|---|
| bn100612038 | 00:55:05.65 | **NOT a GRB** — M2.0 solar flare (GCN 10859 verbatim, our raw file) |
| bn100612545 | 13:04:21 | a REAL GRB — IPN-listed "GRB100612545", seen by INTEGRAL, AGILE, Konus (Hurley+2013 Table 2) |
| **bn100612726** | **17:26:06** | **OURS** |
The literature (Lu+2012, Basak & Rao 2014, Lu+2018 — verified in the PDFs, each printing
the trigger digits next to the name) calls bn100612726 "**GRB 100612A**" — yet by same-day
time order the 13:04 burst should own the "A". Neither burst appears in Greiner's grbgen
table (checked 2026-08-12) and no circular ever assigned letters (n_circulars = 0 for both).
**Rule: identify this burst by trigger number only;** when reading "GRB 100612A" in a paper,
verify it prints 100612726 before harvesting (all papers below were so verified).

## Cross-paper consensus + tensions (Rossi+2026 §4.1 consensus rule applied)
- **Time-averaged Ep:** catalog/fluence frame: 104.5 (Acuner, catalog) / 111 ± 4 (Preece
  Ep,f); peak-flux frame: 143 ± 10 (Preece Ep,p). Peak-bin time-resolved: 115–125 (Yu+2019).
  Consensus fluence-frame **Ep ≈ 105–115 keV** with the frame split recorded — never mix
  Ep,p with Ep,f in a diff.
- **Ep evolution:** HTS in Lu+2012, Basak+2014, Lu+2018 (H), Yu+2019 (h.t.s.); Shao+2017
  reads the same data as "semi-HIT" (initial soft-to-hard, then decay — weak significance
  for this burst). Both recorded; the first-bin Ep is the discriminator (Lu 173; Shao 182;
  Yu 264 at 0.99–1.82 s — binning-dependent).
- **High-energy slope:** catalog β = −3.17 (Acuner row) vs time-resolved Band β −1.9…−2.7
  (Lu+2012) / −2.3…−3.8 (Yu+2019) — steep-β burst, β poorly bounded in several bins
  (Yu+2019's β-degeneracy theme). A P3 watch-item for our BGO-anchored fits.
- **Lags:** Lu+2018 τ̂₃₁ = 0.89 ± 0.08 s; Shao+2017 ΔtHR = 2.656 ± 0.117 s — DIFFERENT
  definitions (CCF lag vs hardness–intensity delay); their Fig. 7 places this burst on the
  ΔtHR–τ correlation. Do not diff one against the other.
- **Thermal component: no paper claims a BB.** Basak & Rao *fit* BBPL alongside Band
  (their Table 2 comparison value 0.73) and report PL-flux "very mild lingering" — the
  closest the literature comes. The P3 diff against our archival 2/8 union-rule BB blocks
  (see P0 freeze) is therefore OURS to make, on OUR terms.
- **Pulse shape:** symmetric (κ = 0.14, Basak; Lu+2012 exception quote) yet HTS — this
  burst breaks the HTS↔asymmetric association in BOTH papers; Basak's Norris fit is poor
  (χ²red 16.8). Useful for our Step-7 pulse work.
