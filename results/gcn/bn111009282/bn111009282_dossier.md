## 2. INSTRUMENT STORY

A **hard, bright, single-pulse GBM burst that nobody circulated.** GBM triggered on
detectors n1/n2/n5 (`DET_MASK`), but the burst is *closest* to **n1 (10.5°)** and
**n0 (15.6°)**; the catalog analysis set is `n0+n1` (von Kienlin `Det = 0+1`).
It is one of the brightest bursts with no GCN at all: fluence
1.20 × 10⁻⁵ erg cm⁻² (10–1000 keV) puts it above the 5 × 10⁻⁶ "bright" cut that
Shao+2017 used to define their sample.

Despite the silence, **four other missions recorded it** (Hurley+2017 IPN supplement
to the 2nd GBM catalog, VizieR `J/ApJS/229/31/table2` row 280):

> `2011 Oct 09 06:45:40   GRB111009282   INT,Kon,MES,Swi   e`

- `INT` = INTEGRAL (SPI-ACS), `Kon` = Konus-Wind, `MES` = MESSENGER, `Swi` = Swift.
- von Kienlin+2014 `OtherDet` column agrees: `K, IA, S, Me`.
- IPN annulus (table 5): centre RA 201.3750, Dec −8.6769, radius 55.6562° ± 0.1242°.
- IPN 3σ error box (table 6): centre **RA 180.255, Dec −62.152**; corners
  (158.836,−52.587) (159.031,−52.327) (207.609,−64.281) (207.805,−64.019);
  **a_GBM = 595 deg², a_IPN = 8.230 deg², ratio 72.3**.
- The IPN box centre lies **5.52° from the GBM position** — inside the GBM 3σ
  contour, and it does not change which detectors are closest (Step-1 QC §4).

⚠ **RECORDED INCONSISTENCY (T5 — do not resolve silently).** Hurley+2017's flag
column carries `e`, whose own Note (4) reads *"Burst was localized by Swift-BAT;
IPN triangulation cannot improve on this localization."* That is contradicted by
(a) von Kienlin+2014 giving `Loc = Fermi-GBM` with a 1.1° radius, (b) tcat
`GCN_FLAG = No`, (c) zero circulars anywhere, and (d) Hurley+2017 nevertheless
printing a full IPN annulus **and** error box for the burst. Either the flag is
mis-set for this row, or a BAT ground-analysis localization exists that was never
circulated. **No arcminute position may be used for this burst** until that is
settled at a primary source. It does not affect any prompt-spectral quantity.

No LAT / LLE: `grb_sample.ecsv` `HAS_LAT = False`, no `gll_*` files, no `LAT/`
directory. Consistent with an Eₚ ≈ 120 keV burst at θ = 36° from the LAT boresight
with no reported >100 MeV emission.

## 4. MULTI-INSTRUMENT CONSENSUS (Phase-4 rule)

or fluence for it** — Konus-Wind's and MESSENGER's detections appear only as IPN

choice** (Eₚ = 122.4 keV in the 1.02-s peak slice; 207 → 54 keV across Lu+2018's 9 bins),
not cross-calibration. Any correlation-plane placement must carry that interval label.
