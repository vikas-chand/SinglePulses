## Instrument story

Exactly **one** GCN circular exists for this burst — **GCN 24038** (Insight-HXMT/HE
detection, Luo et al., 2019-04-01 13:52 UT). No Fermi-GBM team circular was issued; no
Swift, no localization refinements, no afterglow searches. From GCN 24038 (verbatim):

> "At 2019-04-01T03:20:21.000 (T0), the Insight-HXMT/HE detected GRB 190401A
> (trigger ID: HEB190401139) in a routine search of the data, which was also triggered
> by INTEGRAL (trigger_num:8247) and Fermi/GBM (trigger_num:575781625)."

> "The Insight-HXMT/HE light curve mainly consists of one pulse with a duration (T90)
> of 23.50 s measured from T0-1.64 s. The 1-ms peak rate, measured from T0+0.690 s,
> is 1193 cnts/sec. The total counts from this burst is 11740 counts."

HXMT band: "CsI detectors operating in the regular mode with the energy range of about
80-800 keV (record energy)"; only >~200 keV photons penetrate to the CsI (GCN 24038).

So the observing record is: **Fermi/GBM (trigger) + INTEGRAL SPI-ACS (trigger 8247) +
Insight-HXMT/HE (blind-search detection)**. GBM-only for spectroscopy.
