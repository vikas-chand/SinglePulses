**Component-coverage note (T9), stated once and inherited by every diff below.** This burst is
one emission complex — no precursor, no separate episode, `T90START = +1.54 s` (so **no
pre-trigger emission**, unlike the D4 bursts). But the three external frames cover *different
fractions of the same complex*:

| frame | interval | fraction of the T90 span |
|---|---|---|
| our stamped source window | **[−1.548, +30.618] s** | ≈ 36 % of T90; contains ALL of T50 [4.86, 28.93] |
| GBM spectral catalog `flnc` | [−2.048, +60.416] s | ≈ 75 % |
| bcat T90 | [1.54, 84.23] s | 100 % |
| Lu+2018 time-resolved | [−0.83, +16.30] s | ≈ 18 % |
| Shao+2017 CCF / lag window | (−0.5, 25.0) s effective (their Table 1 binsize 1.00 s) | ≈ 29 % |

So **our window is the bright core plus the first third of the tail**. Because the Ep evolution
is monotonically hard-to-soft (below), *any* Ep diffed against the catalog's longer `flnc`
interval must expect the catalog to be **softer** than ours by construction. This is a frame
difference, never a discrepancy.

## PUBLISHED VALUES (for the P3 diff) — latest-first (Phase 3b), frame-aligned (Phase 4)

### A. Lu, Yang, Zou et al. 2018 — *Comprehensive Analysis of Fermi GRB Data IV* (2018ApJ...865..153L) — NEWEST, the primary comparison frame
Frame (their §2): GBM **TTE from the brightest NaI** (smallest source angle); light curves via
gtBurst + HEASOFT 6.20; spectra with **RMFIT 4.3.2**, **Band function**, bins built at **SNR > 40**;
statistic reported as χ²ᵣ. Component coverage = the single pulse, [−0.83, +16.30] s.

**Table 2 — time-resolved Band fits, verbatim block (p.42 of the arXiv PDF):**

| Timebin (s) | Amplitude | **Ep (keV)** | α | β | Flux (erg s⁻¹ cm⁻²) | χ²ᵣ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| −0.83 – 3.68 | 0.02 ± 0.00 | **326.72 ± 54.68** | −0.55 ± 0.12 | −1.93 ± 0.12 | 1.24E−06 ± 4.28E−08 | 0.97 |
| 3.68 – 5.68 | 0.07 ± 0.02 | **186.22 ± 25.03** | −0.28 ± 0.16 | −2.11 ± 0.15 | 1.53E−06 ± 6.18E−08 | 0.95 |
| 5.68 – 9.11 | 0.06 ± 0.02 | **151.78 ± 18.31** | −0.32 ± 0.16 | −2.31 ± 0.21 | 9.32E−07 ± 4.82E−08 | 0.95 |
| 9.11 – 16.30 | 0.05 ± 0.02 | **103.45 ± 14.62** | −0.29 ± 0.23 | −2.15 ± 0.14 | 5.11E−07 ± 3.02E−08 | 1.06 |

Ep falls **monotonically 327 → 186 → 152 → 103 keV** while the flux *rises then falls*
(1.24 → 1.53 → 0.93 → 0.51 e−6). α is flat at ≈ −0.3 to −0.55; β is stable at ≈ −2.0 to −2.3
(a genuine, well-measured high-energy power law). **Convention:** `Ep` here is the νFν peak of
the Band function; α, β are photon indices in the usual (negative) sign.

**Table 1 — Kocevski et al. 2003 pulse fit (their Eq. 1), brightest NaI:**
`bn110605183 NaI  Im = 51.97 ± 2.02 · tm = 3.77 ± 0.19 s · r = 1.96 ± 0.22 · d = 1.38 ± 0.15 · t0 = 1.95 · χ²ᵣ = 1.14`
⚠ **T3 (definitions travel with numbers):** `t0` is *not* a free shape parameter — Lu fixes it at
the time of 0.1·Im on the RISE, then fits; `r`/`d` are the power-law rise/decay indices and are
**degenerate with t0**, so they are only comparable to another fit that adopts the same t0 rule.

**Table 3 — spectral lags (CCF vs the 8–25 keV reference band; MC-Gaussian errors), window (−0.5, 25.0) s:**
| band | range | τ̂ (s) |
|---|---|---|
| ② | 50–100 keV (NaI) | **0.85 ± 0.49** |
| ③ | 100–300 keV (NaI) | **1.73 ± 0.41** |
| ⑤ | 300–1000 keV (**BGO**) | **2.48 ± 0.41** |

**Sign convention (verbatim §2):** τ̂ is *"the spectral lag with respect to 8–25 keV"*; τ̂ grows
with energy here, i.e. **positive lag = soft photons arrive later** — the classic long-GRB sense.

**Table 4:** `bn110605183 | Evolution = H | τ̂₃₁ = 1.48 ± 0.42 s | W = 21.95 | k_τ̂ = 2.03 ± 0.79 | kE = −0.77 ± 0.14`
- `H` = **hard-to-soft (H2S)** Ep evolution — *classified by eye* (their §3.1; a judgement, not a fit).
- `τ̂₃₁` = lag between **100–300 and 25–50 keV**, the convention used in the older literature.
- `W` = **FWHM of the pulse in 8 keV–1 MeV** (their §3.2). ⚠ this is *not* the same `W` as
  Lu's own §3.1 pulse-shape discussion, which defines `W = T_d + T_r` at the 10 % level
  (**T3 again — the same symbol, two definitions, one paper**).
- `kE` from `log Ep = kE·log(t + t0) + b`; `kE = −0.77 ± 0.14` → **well inside the H2S class**
  (they require kE < −0.2) and close to the sample centre (−0.8).
- `k_τ̂` from `τ̂ = k_τ̂·log E + b`.

### Multi-instrument consensus (Phase 4 / Rossi+2026 procedure)

of them published a spectrum for it** (searched: ADS four-form + supplementary forms + a web

## BEYOND-PROMPT CONTEXT (future modules)

multimessenger association — zero circulars means no follow-up ever happened. The only
non-prompt-adjacent tag in this burst's record is the IPN/multi-mission detection list

## What this burst is FOR, in our program
1. **A clean H2S single pulse with a large, well-measured lag** (τ̂₃₁ = 1.48 ± 0.42 s, W = 21.95 s)
   — it sits on the published τ̂₃₁–W relation and is a natural test object for the lag–MVT and
   HLE work.
2. **An unbiased BB/two-break test case.** Nobody has ever fit anything but Band/CPL/SBPL to it,
   and nobody has ever proposed a photosphere for it. Whatever our union rule and DSBPL do here
   is an **unpublished decision**, to be scored against the expert band rather than reconciled
   against a paper (see `notes/reconciliation/bn110605183_P0_frozen.json`).
3. **A detector-selection corroboration.** The GBM spectral catalog independently chose
   b0 + n2 + n5 — our approved set exactly — and Shao+2017 independently chose n2, our brightest.
