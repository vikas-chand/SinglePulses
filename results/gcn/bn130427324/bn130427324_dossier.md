## 3. Redshift — three independent measurements, all consistent

> **GCN 14455 (Gemini-N/GMOS, Levan+):** *"we identify absorption lines due to Ca H and K,
> Mg I as well as the Mg II doublet at a common redshift of **z=0.34**. We suggest this to be
> the redshift of GRB 130427A."* (absorption only; no host emission seen)

> **GCN 14478 (NOT/ALFOSC, Xu+):** *"We detect prominent absorption lines of the MgII doublet,
> MgI and CaII K & H as well as weak emission lines of the [OII] doublet and H-beta, all at a
> common redshift of **z=0.338+/-0.002**, consistent with the redshift reported by Levan et al."*

> **GCN 14491 (VLT/X-shooter, Flores+):** *"several absorption features including FeII, MnII,
> MgII, MgI, TiII, CaII, NaI, and emission lines such as H-alpha, H-beta, [OIII], and [OII],
> all at a common redshift of **z=0.3399 +/- 0.0002**"*

**Adopted: z = 0.3399 ± 0.0002** (X-shooter — highest resolution, both absorption AND host
emission lines). No ambiguity to record (contrast the 110721A two-value lesson).
**Associated SN 2013cq** — spectroscopic detection reported in GCN 14646 (GTC 10.4 m) and
published by Xu+2013 (`2013ApJ...776...98X`); HST follow-up GCN 14686.

## 4. Instrument story (what the world saw, in GBM time)

- **GBM** triggered at T0 = 07:47:06.42 on the *first* pulse and issued an **Autonomous Repoint
  Request** on the strength of BGO emission (GCN 14473). Light curve, verbatim: *"a bright
  structured peak followed at ~T0+120 s by a FRED-like pulse. The overall duration (T90) is
  about 138s s (50-300 keV)."* **That FRED-like pulse at ~T0+120 s IS our analysis window.**
- **LAT**: 47.3° off-axis at T0, ARR brought it to 20.1°, in the FoV for **715 s** before Earth
  occultation, re-emerging at T0+3135 s (Ackermann+2014). >500 photons >100 MeV in 80 ks;
  highest-energy photon **95 GeV at T0 + 244 s** (Ackermann+2014; 94 GeV in GCN 14471; Ajello+2019
  Sect. 4.8 says 94.1 GeV and that 130427A "holds the record with 17 photons" above threshold).
- **LLE**: *"thousands of counts above background … within a 100 s interval coinciding with the
  time of the GBM emission, with a significance of ~40 sigma"* (GCN 14471). Bissaldi+2015
  Table II gives σ_LLE(<100 MeV) = **40.3**; Maraventano+2025 quotes **15σ** from Ajello+2019
  Table 2 — a definition/interval difference, recorded as such, not resolved here.
- **Swift/BAT** triggered 51 s late (image trigger) because the main emission arrived during a
  pre-planned slew; BAT T90(15–350 keV) = **162.83 ± 1.36 s** in BAT frame (GCN 14470).
- **Konus-Wind, SPI-ACS/INTEGRAL, RHESSI, MAXI/GSC, AGILE (GRID + MCAL)** all detected it.
  RHESSI explicitly imaged our pulse: *"The lightcurve of the slower, fainter peak of the GRB,
  about two minutes after the primary outburst"* (GCN 14590) — RHESSI data are public and
  usable 50 keV–15 MeV.
- **RAPTOR** caught the optical flash R ≈ 7.4 *before* the Swift trigger, i.e. contemporaneous
  with the GBM/LAT onset (GCN 14476, clarified in GCN 14508).
