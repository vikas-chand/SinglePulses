### P3-diff cautions raised at harvest (record, don't resolve)
- **Ep spread across frames is real and frame-driven:** 192±16 (GBM circ, −1.5→25.1 s) / 215±48
  (KW, +2.9→43.6 s, 20 keV–10 MeV) / 149±15 (Ghosh −1→40 s, includes the β-flattening soft tail) /
  201±19 (Ghosh −1→15 s) / 266.6±34 (Chang 0→30 s). Interval AND band differ everywhere — apply
  L21 frame alignment before calling any of it a discrepancy. Note Ghosh's (−1,40) β = −1.60 ±
  0.02 is much flatter than the circulars' — their own (20,40) bin is PL −1.34, i.e. the soft/HLE
  tail drags the time-integrated β; our stamped window ends at +25.9 s and should be compared to
  frames 1/7, not 6.
- **Ghosh internal inconsistencies (T5), all recorded:** (a) Eiso 1.80e53 (Table 1) vs 3.04e53
  (Ep,i–Eiso section); (b) fluence error ±0.05 (Table 1) vs ±0.03 (text; = GCN 27809 value);
  (c) LC/temporal detector list "NaI 0, 1, 3, and 5 ... BGO 1" (§2) vs spectral detector list
  "NaI_00, NaI_01, NaI_03, BGO_00 (≤60°)" (§ spectral analysis); (d) Table 1 lists "Ep = 291 ±
  25 keV" as THE burst property — that is the (4,5) s peak-bin CPL Ep, not the time-integrated Ep
  (149). A naive Table-1 harvest would mislabel the component (T9).
- **XRT decay:** GCN 27811 automated α = 3.42 (+0.06, −2.83) vs Ghosh+2026 α = −0.57 (+0.05,
  −0.03) (their sign convention f ∝ t^α). Different epochs/fitting of a sparse PC-mode light
  curve; the GCN's own error bar spans nearly 0 — prefer the refereed re-analysis (Phase 3b).
- **LAT highest photon:** 9.2 GeV at T0+748 s (GCN 27797) vs 9.213 GeV at ~T0+745 s (Ghosh+2026)
  — same photon, refined numbers.
