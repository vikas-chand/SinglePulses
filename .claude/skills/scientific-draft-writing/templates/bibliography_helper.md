# Bibliography helper

For every citation, record its role:

```text
foundational model
primary observational result
method/statistic
instrument or catalog
contrasting interpretation
review
software
```

Prefer primary sources for equations and methods. Use reviews to orient the reader, not to replace the source of a quantitative claim.

Before submission verify:

```text
all cited works in bibliography
all bibliography entries cited
correct author/year
correct DOI/journal
no citation attached to a claim it does not support
balanced representation of competing models
```
