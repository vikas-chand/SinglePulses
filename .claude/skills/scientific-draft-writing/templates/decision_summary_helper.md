# Decision-summary paragraph helper

Use:

```text
Decision:
Evidence:
Competing alternatives:
Assumptions:
Uncertainty/non-identifiability:
Next discriminating test:
```

Paragraph pattern:

> Under [criterion and assumptions], the data favor [decision] because [evidence]. [Alternative] remains [allowed/disfavored] owing to [reason]. The conclusion is most sensitive to [assumption]. [Test] would distinguish the remaining interpretations.
