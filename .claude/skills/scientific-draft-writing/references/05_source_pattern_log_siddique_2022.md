# Source pattern log: Siddique et al. (2022)

This log records transferable writing moves, not copied prose.

## Introduction

### Move: uncertainty linked to system composition

The paper does not leave “prompt mechanism unknown” as an isolated fact. It explains that the answer changes the inferred jet composition.

Use when a technical ambiguity has broader physical consequences.

### Move: fair taxonomy of alternatives

The introduction gives fireball/internal shocks, magnetic/reconnection, dissipative photosphere, and hybrid scenarios before narrowing to the selected frameworks.

Use to show that the analysis is discriminating among live models rather than confirming one favorite.

### Move: capability sentence

The joint GBM+LAT range is described before the model list, explaining why the data can separate components.

## Observations

### Move: event timeline

Trigger, localization, viewing angle, high-energy events, extended emission, follow-up, and redshift are presented before detailed analysis.

## Methods

### Move: exclusion with rationale

A detector is reported as initially included, then excluded because of a detector-specific residual trend and likely blockage.

### Move: method ladder

Data selection -> backgrounds/responses -> LAT likelihood -> joint models -> selection rule -> preferred model.

## Results

### Move: report parameter movement

The paper states how adding BB changes the continuum slope and peak energy, not only the fit statistic.

### Move: summary table + appendix audit

The main table contains preferred models; appendices contain every attempted fit and failed convergence.

## Discussion

### Move: assumptions before inference

The paper states that its frameworks assume no subphotospheric dissipation, a photospheric BB, and a nonthermal Band/SBPL continuum before deriving outflow properties.

### Move: physical-question headings

The conclusion is divided into pure fireball, initial magnetic fraction, magnetization at larger radius, end of acceleration, Lorentz factor/photospheric radius, and comparison sources.

## Figures

- Component SEDs with uncertainty bands.
- Total and BB flux evolution.
- Parameter maps over launch radius and efficiency.
- Direct comparison of two physical frameworks.
