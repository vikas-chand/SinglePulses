# Writing Pattern Log: Lu et al. (2018)

## 1. Position one paper inside a series

The final introduction paragraph briefly states what the earlier papers accomplished before identifying the new problem.

Use when a manuscript extends an established program:

```text
Prior work established A and B.
A subsequent study examined C.
Here we address D.
```

This gives continuity without re-summarizing every earlier result.

## 2. Research-roadmap paragraph

The paragraph then maps:

```text
sample and reduction
observational relations
simulations and origin
summary
```

A roadmap is useful when the paper has a clear sequence from data to phenomenology to physical interpretation.

## 3. Scope-boundary sentence

The paper notes that pulse asymmetry may be connected to spectral evolution but explicitly says that the present work focuses on lag.

Reusable move:

> Although X may also affect the observable, here we focus on Y.

This is not an apology. It is narrative control.

## 4. Confounder before inference

Triple-peaked residual structure is introduced before the authors interpret tracking behavior.

Strong order:

```text
observation
possible confounder
sample restriction
main inference
```

## 5. Empirical fit clearly labeled

Relations such as `tau ∝ log E` summarize data. They should be described as empirical parameterizations, not physical laws.

## 6. Discussion from correlation to mechanism

The conclusion moves from measured correlations to phenomenological simulations, then contrasts two physical scenarios and explains why one is easier to realize.

Use this structure:

```text
what is observed
what minimal simulation reproduces
what physical pictures could realize it
which assumptions make one picture difficult
what new test could distinguish them
```

## 7. Writing caution learned from the source

Even a well-framed paper can contain local inconsistencies in units, labels, and definitions. A writing helper must verify every symbol against equations, tables, and figure captions rather than copying prose uncritically.
