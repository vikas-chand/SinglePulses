==========
References
==========

The cited PDFs are **not redistributed** in this repository; the full BibTeX is
in ``paper/two_break.bib``. Each entry below notes its role in this analysis.

Instruments, methods & tools
============================

* **Meegan et al. 2009**, ApJ 702, 791 — Fermi/GBM instrument.
* **Atwood et al. 2009**, ApJ 697, 1071 — Fermi/LAT (LLE data).
* **Vianello et al. 2015**, arXiv:1507.08343 — **3ML**, the fitting framework.
* **Scargle et al. 2013**, ApJ 764, 167 — **Bayesian Blocks** (time binning).
* **Akaike 1974**, IEEE TAC 19, 716 / **Schwarz 1978**, Ann. Stat. 6, 461 —
  **AIC / BIC** model selection.
* **Wilks 1938**, Ann. Math. Stat. 9, 60 — likelihood-ratio test (nested pairs
  only).
* **D'Agostini 2005**, arXiv:physics/0511182 — errors-in-both-variables
  correlation fit.
* **Busby & Lazzati 2024**, ApJ 972, 83 — single-pulse "horizontal-line"
  selection.
* **Kaneko et al. 2006**, ApJS 166, 298; **Gruber et al. 2014**, ApJS 211, 12;
  **Yu et al. 2016**, A&A 588, A135 — GBM time-resolved spectral catalogues
  (comparison + SBPL smoothness).

Spectral models
===============

* **Band et al. 1993**, ApJ 413, 281 — the Band function.
* **Ravasio et al. 2018**, A&A 613, A16; **Ravasio et al. 2019**, A&A 625, A60 —
  **2SBPL** functional form + smoothness (:math:`n_1{=}5.38,\ n_2{=}2.69`) and
  the synchrotron-break interpretation.
* **Guiriec et al. 2010** (ApJ 725, 225), **2011** (ApJL 727, L33), **2015**
  (ApJ 807, 148) — multi-component (non-thermal + blackbody) decomposition.
* **Sari, Piran & Narayan 1998**, ApJL 497, L17 — synchrotron
  cooling/injection breaks.
* **Preece et al. 1998**, ApJL 506, L23; **Crider et al. 1997**, ApJL 479, L39 —
  synchrotron "lines of death" (:math:`-2/3,\ -3/2`).

Science comparison (single-pulse / curvature)
=============================================

* **Burgess et al. 2014a**, ApJL 784, L43 — the :math:`E_{\rm p}`–:math:`kT`
  correlation we test at scale.
* **Burgess et al. 2014b**, ApJ 784, 17 — physical synchrotron fits to single
  pulses.
* **Burgess et al. 2019**, MNRAS 490, 927 — Bayesian short-GRB catalogue
  (Bayes-factor threshold).
* **Basak & Rao 2013** (MNRAS 436, 3082), **2014** (MNRAS 442, 419) —
  single-pulse BBPL/2BBPL, HTS/IT.
* **Lu et al. 2012**, ApJ 756, 112 — HTS/IT :math:`E_{\rm p}`-evolution classes.
* **Ryde 2004** (ApJ 614, 827); **Ryde & Pe'er 2009** (ApJ 702, 1211) —
  photospheric BBPL lineage.
* **Oganesyan et al. 2017** (ApJ 846, 137), **2018** (A&A 616, A138) —
  low-energy synchrotron breaks.
* **Ravasio et al. 2023**, arXiv:2303.16223 — 2SBPL second break only at the
  highest S/N (GRB 221009A).
* **Ronchi et al. 2020**, A&A 636, A55 — time-resolved physical synchrotron
  (:math:`E_c`, :math:`E_m` co-evolution).
* **Mei et al. 2025**, A&A 693, A156 — :math:`\nu_c`–:math:`L_{\rm iso}` vs no
  :math:`E_{\rm p}`–:math:`L`; cooling-regime ratio.
* **Acuner et al. 2020**, ApJ 893, 128 — when :math:`\alpha` discriminates
  photosphere vs synchrotron.
* **Li et al. 2021**, ApJS 254, 35 — the writing-style + selection-threshold
  template.
* **Mészáros & Rees 2000** (ApJ 530, 292); **Pe'er et al. 2007** (ApJL 664, L1);
  **Kumar & Zhang 2015** (Phys. Rep. 561, 1); **Yonetoku et al. 2004** (ApJ 609,
  935) — photosphere theory, review, Yonetoku relation.
